Once in a generation, a divinely chosen ruler is born amongst the people. Some cults and countries seek out these blessed rulers, trusting the gods to select their leader. However, the existence of these chosen Sorcerers can pose a significant threat to those currently in power. While some will willingly step aside to let the will of the gods rule, other less honorable leaders will hunt down these challengers to their power.

As a Royal Sorcerer, you are granted great power, but with that power also comes responsibility. Will you lead the your people to destruction or into a new golden age?

## Subclass Features

### Royal Bearing (Level 1)

Your Sorcery infuses you with supernatural bearing. Choose Deception, Persuasion, or Intimidation. You gain proficiency in that skill, and you add double your proficiency bonus to ability checks you make with the chosen skill.

### Words of Authority (Level 1)

Your voice has been imbued with sorcerous authority, and it is difficult for the weak to resist your commands. You learn the Heightened Spell Metamagic. It does not count against your Metamagics Known, and it cannot be replaced.

When you cast an enchantment spell targeting a creature within 10 feet of you, you can apply Heightened Spell at no extra cost. Once you apply Heightened Spell in this way, you must finish a short or long rest before you can do so again.

### Inspiring Sorcery (Level 6)

You can lace your words with sorcerous power to uplift your allies. As a bonus action, you can expend Sorcery Points and choose a number of creatures who can see or hear you within 30 feet equal to the Sorcery Points expended. Creatures gain temporary hit points equal to 1d4 + your Charisma modifier.

### Regal Presence (Level 14)

Your presence inspires heroic confidence in your followers. Creatures of your choice that can see or hear you within 30 feet are considered to be proficient in all saving throws.

Also, the range of Words of Authority becomes 30 feet.

### Divine Command (Level 18)

*18th-level Royal feature*

You have become a legendary figure, and have been infused with increased divine power. You learn [divine word](/spells/divine-word-xphb.md). It counts as a Sorcerer spell for you, but it does not count against your total Spells Known, and you cannot replace it.

Once between each long rest, you can cast [divine word](/spells/divine-word-xphb.md) without expending your Sorcery Points or one of your Innate Arcanum spell slots.
