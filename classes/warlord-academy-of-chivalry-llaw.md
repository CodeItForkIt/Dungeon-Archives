Warlords that choose the Academy of Chivalry often hail from the ranks of nobility and are almost always formal knights. As leaders, they hold themselves and those who follow them to a lofty code of conduct. These knights stand as beacons of fair play, honor, and justice, both on and off the field of battle.

## Subclass Features

### Chivalric Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the Warlord levels noted in the table below. They do not count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level:

| Warlord Level | Exploit |
|---------------|---------|
| 3rd | [attack order](/optional-features/attack-order-llexploits.md), [taunting strike](/optional-features/taunting-strike-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md), [rejuvenating order](/optional-features/rejuvenating-order-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [daring rescue](/optional-features/daring-rescue-llexploits.md) |
^warlord-level-exploit

Options (choose 2):

- [attack order](/optional-features/attack-order-llexploits.md)  
- [taunting strike](/optional-features/taunting-strike-llexploits.md)  

### Inspiring Shout (Level 3)

Your presence on the field of battle inspires those who fight beside you. When you roll initiative you can expend a use of Inspiring Word and choose a number of creatures within 30 feet that can hear you equal to your Leadership modifier to gain temporary hit points equal to your Exploit Die + your Leadership modifier.

### Knighthood (Level 3)

You have been trained in the classical skills of knighthood. You gain proficiency with all martial weapons, one musical instrument, and either [History](/rules/skills.md#History), [Performance](/rules/skills.md#Performance), or [Religion](/rules/skills.md#Religion).

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md)  
- [rejuvenating order](/optional-features/rejuvenating-order-llexploits.md)  

### Lead the Charge (Level 6)

You lead best from the front lines. When you take the [Attack](/rules/actions.md#Attack) action on your turn and make at least one melee attack, you can issue an [attack order](/optional-features/attack-order-llexploits.md) as a bonus action on that turn.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [daring rescue](/optional-features/daring-rescue-llexploits.md)  

### Flames of Hope (Level 14)

Once between each short or long rest you can use your action to let forth a heroic cry that inflames the hearts of your allies and inspires terror in your foes. Creatures that can hear you within 30 feet gain the corresponding effect below:

**Allies.** Friendly creatures gain temporary hit points equal to your Exploit Die + your Leadership modifier. While these last, the creature cannot be [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [Frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened).

**Enemies.** Hostile creatures must make a Wisdom saving throw or be [Frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) of you. They repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns and when they take damage, ending the effect on a success.

### Paragon of Chivalry (Level 18)

You stand as an exemplar of chivalric virtue, a shining beacon for those who follow you. You are immune to the [Frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) and [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed) conditions, and creatures of your choice within 30 feet that can see you have advantage on saving throws to resist or end the Charmed and Frightened condition.
