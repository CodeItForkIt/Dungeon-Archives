No matter the mission, a Guerrilla will execute it with utmost perfection. Sometimes known as commandos, these warriors are marked by their determination and adaptability. Whether harsh terrain, vicious monsters, enemy soldiers, or powerful spellcasters, nothing short of death will cause the willpower of a Guerrilla warrior to waver in the pursuit of their goals.

## Subclass Features

### Adaptable Combatant (Level 3)

You have honed your skills to meet any challenge. You gain proficiency in [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics), [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception), [Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth), or [Survival](/rules/skills.md#Survival). If you choose a skill you are proficient in, you instead gain a bonus to checks with that skill equal to your Exploit Die.

Moreover, during the course of a long rest, you can spend 1 hour performing combat drills and practicing techniques. At the end of this hour, you can replace any number of your current Exploits Known with other Martial Exploits of your choice, so long as you meet the new Exploit prerequisites.

### Guerrilla Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the Fighter levels noted in the table below. They don't count against your total number of Exploits Known and cannot be switched for other Exploits:

**Guerrilla Exploits**

| Fighter Level | Exploits |
|---------------|----------|
| 3rd | [first aid](/optional-features/first-aid-llexploits.md), [mighty leap](/optional-features/mighty-leap-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [crippling strike](/optional-features/crippling-strike-llexploits.md), [pinning shot](/optional-features/pinning-shot-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [survey wilderness](/optional-features/survey-wilderness-llexploits.md) |
^guerrilla-exploits

### Survivalist (Level 3)

You are an expert at overcoming natural obstacles. You learn either the [Mariner](/optional-features/mariner-llstyles.md) or [Mountaineer](/optional-features/mountaineer-llstyles.md) Fighting Style. It does not count against your total number of Fighting Styles, but you cannot replace it with another Fighting Style.

### By Land or Sea (Level 7)

You have trained to find success in any environment, either it be land, sea, or sky. You gain the benefits listed below:

- At the end of each long rest you can change switch your Fighting Style from Survivalist for the other option.  
- You can hold your breath for up to 1 hour.  
- You ignore the effects of nonmagical difficult terrain.  
- Whenever you use your [Second Wind](/classes/alternate-fighter-llaf.md#Second%20Wind%20(Level%201)) you also gain the benefits of the [Dash](/rules/actions.md#Dash) action or the [mighty leap](/optional-features/mighty-leap-llexploits.md) Exploit.  

### Identify Weakness (Level 10)

You pinpoint your enemy's weaknesses and ruthlessly punish them. When you succeed on an [Eye for Talent](/classes/alternate-fighter-llaf.md#Eye%20for%20Talent%20(Level%203)) check against a creature you can forgo the normal knowledge you would gain, and instead, the next time you force it to make a saving throw against one of your Exploits it does so with disadvantage.

### Unwavering (Level 15)

You are remarkably hardy, even amongst elite warriors. Each time you use [Second Wind](/classes/alternate-fighter-llaf.md#Second%20Wind%20(Level%201)) you regain the maximum amount of hit points and you regain one expended Exploit Die.

You can also expend your [Action Surge](/classes/alternate-fighter-llaf.md#Action%20Surge%20(Level%206)) to instantly replace one of your Exploits with another Exploit you could learn.

### Legendary Guerrilla (Level 18)

There is nothing that will stand between you and your goals when they are within your reach. When you roll initiative and are not Surprised, you gain all of the following benefits:

- You gain temporary hit points equal to your Fighter level.  
- You can take the [Search](/rules/actions.md#Search) action to use [Eye for Talent](/classes/alternate-fighter-llaf.md#Eye%20for%20Talent%20(Level%203)).  
- You can immediately move up to your full speed without provoking [opportunity attacks](/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack).
