## Hit Points

- **Hit Dice**: 1d10 per Shifter level
- **Hit Points at First Level:** 10 + CON
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** add 6 OR 1d10 + CON  (minimum of 1)

## Starting Shifter

- **Saving Throw Proficiencies**: Dexterity, Strength
- **Skill Proficiencies**: *Choose 2:* [Acrobatics](/rules/skills.md#Acrobatics), [Animal Handling](/rules/skills.md#Animal%20Handling), [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics), [Nature](/rules/skills.md#Nature), [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception), [Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth), or [Survival](/rules/skills.md#Survival)
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Simple weapons, [blowguns](/items/blowgun-xphb.md), [longbows](/items/longbow-xphb.md), and [nets](/items/net-xphb.md)
- **Armor Training**: [Light armor](/rules/item-types.md#Light%20Armor)

**Starting Equipment:** You start with the following items, plus anything provided by your background.

- *(a)* two [clubs](/items/club-xphb.md), *(b)* a [greatclub](/items/greatclub-xphb.md), or *(c)* a [quarterstaff](/items/quarterstaff-xphb.md)
- *(a)* a [shortbow](/items/shortbow-xphb.md) and 20 [arrows](/items/arrow-xphb.md), or *(b)* four [javelins](/items/javelin-xphb.md)
- [leather armor](/items/leather-armor-xphb.md), a [dagger](/items/dagger-xphb.md), and an [explorer's pack](/items/explorers-pack-xphb.md)

Alternatively, you may start with `2d4 × 10` gp to buy your own equipment.

## Multiclassing Shifter

**Ability Score Minimum:** Constitution 13

When you gain a level in a class other than your first, you gain only some of that class's starting proficiencies.

- **Skill Proficiencies**: *Choose 1:* [Acrobatics](/rules/skills.md#Acrobatics), [Animal Handling](/rules/skills.md#Animal%20Handling), [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics), [Nature](/rules/skills.md#Nature), [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception), [Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth), or [Survival](/rules/skills.md#Survival)
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Simple weapons

## Class Features

### Bloodline (Level 1)

At 1st level, you choose the Bloodline from the list below that best represents your primal abilities and favored wild Beasts.

Your Bloodline grants you features at 1st level, and again when you reach 7th, 13th, and 18th level in this class. Your Bloodline features all use your Shifter save DC (see below).

**Bloodline Shapes.** Each Bloodline has an instinctive connection to certain types of Beasts; these are known as their Bloodline Shapes. Beast stat blocks in the Compendium of Beasts ^[creature/LaserLlama; Compendium of Beasts.json] will include which Bloodline (or Bloodlines) they belong to.

For other Beasts, work with your DM to determine which of the seven Bloodlines, if any, other Beasts may belong to. 

> [!note] Magical Taxonomy
> 
> While animals in the real world are organized into a complex taxonomy, there are only six Bloodlines that the mystical powers of a Shifter use: *Aquatic*, *Avian*, *Brute*, *Carnivore*, *Insect*, *Reptilian*, and *Vermin*.
> 
> Though, legends speak of Shifters who call on the powers of an *Ancient Bloodline* of Beasts...
^magical-taxonomy

### Wild Shape (Level 1)

Also at 1st level, you gain the mystical ability to change your form, Shifting into Beast Shapes, following the rules below:

**Beast Shapes.** At 1st level, you know one Beast Shape of your choice of CR 0 or lower from your Bloodline Shapes. As a bonus action, you can Shift to take the form of a Beast Shape you know.

When you Shift, your statistics are replaced by those of the Beast Shape, but you retain your alignment, personality, _hit points_, and Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores. You also retain all of your skill and saving throw proficiencies, in addition to gaining those of the Beast. If you and your Beast Shape both share a proficiency, use the higher bonus.

You revert to your normal form if you are Incapacitated or if you use your bonus action to do so. You can Shift from one Beast Shape directly into another. However, after you spend 1 hour in Beast Shapes without reverting to your normal form, you will instantly revert to your normal form.

**Diminutive Beasts.** Some of the Beast Shapes referenced by this class are a new creature size: _Diminutive_. If you take _any_ damage or you fail a saving throw while Shifted into a Diminutive Beast Shape, you instantly revert back to your normal form.

**Equipment.** When you Shift, you can choose for any natural or unrefined equipment, such as objects made from wood, animal parts, or other non-refined material, to merge with your Beast Shape.

However, any equipment made from refined metal or other highly processed materials, such as metal weapons or armor, falls to the ground in your space when you Shift.

**Features & Spellcasting.** When you Shift, you retain the benefits of features from your class, race, and other sources, and can still use them so long as that Beast is physically capable of doing so. However, you lose all the benefits from special senses, such as Darkvision, unless your Beast Shape also has that sense.

You can't cast spells in Beast Shape, but you can maintain your concentration on spells that you cast before you Shifted.

**Mystic Empowerment.** The primal power in your blood draws out the full potential of your Beasts. When you Shift, you can use the Shifter save DC and Shifter attack modifier in place of the Beast's innate save DC and its attack modifier, unless the Beast's bonus is higher. They use the Constitution modifeir of your normal form:

<span class='abilityDc'>**Shifter save DC save DC**: Constitution + Proficiency Bonus</span>

<span class='abilityAttackMod'>**Shifter attack modifier attack modifier**: Constitution + Proficiency Bonus</span>

**Natural Armor.** Unless your Beast Shape's AC was already higher, you can use the Natural Armor AC from your Bloodline while in Beast Shape. If you do so, use your normal form's ability scores.

**Learning New Beast Shapes.** At certain Shifter levels, you instinctively learn to Shift into more powerful Beasts, as shown in the Max. CR column of the Shifter table. Every time your Max. CR increases, you instinctively learn one Bloodline Shape of your choice equal to that new CR, or lower.

### Primal Bond (Level 2)

At 2nd level, your mystical bond with the wild grows. You can verbally communicate with and understand Beasts. This does not mean Beasts are automatically Friendly to you. When you communicate with a Beast, its awareness and ability to recall knowledge is limited by its Intelligence score.

You can also draw upon this bond to learn additional Beast Shapes. As an action, you can touch a live, non-Hostile Beast within your Max. CR limit, and learn its Beast Shape. There is no limit to the number of Beast Shapes you can learn.

### Bestial Instincts (Level 3)

Your instincts allow you to quickly react to danger. Beginning at 3rd level, so long as you are not Incapacitated or Surprised, you have advantage on your initiative rolls, and when you roll initiative, you can Shift into a Beast Shape.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 4)

At 4th level, you can increase one of your ability scores by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You cannot use this feature to increase any ability score above 20. You gain this feature again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

### Feral Warrior (Level 5)

At 5th level, the power in your blood awakens the ferocity that lay dormant within you, granting you the following benefits:

- Whenever you use your action to Dash or Disengage, you can make one Natural Weapon attack as a bonus action.  
- When you take the Attack action in your normal form, you can make two Natural Weapon attacks instead of one.  
- When you are in Beast Shape, you can use two Actions in the Beast's statblock instead of one. If the Beast has the Multiattack action, you can only use it once per turn.  

### Mystical Strikes (Level 5)

Also at 5th level, your Natural Weapon attacks count as magical for overcoming Resistances and Immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

### Adrenaline Surge (Level 6)

Starting at 6th level, you can draw on your primal drive to survive in the midst of battle. Whenever you take damage, you can use a reaction to gain temporary hit points equal to the damage taken. However, these temporary hit points only last for 1 minute.

You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your normal form's Constitution modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all uses at the end of each short or long rest.

### Bloodline Feature (Level 7)

You gain a feature from your Bloodline.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 8)

At 8th level, you can increase one of your ability scores by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You cannot use this feature to increase any ability score above 20.

### Wild Awareness (Level 9)

At 9th level, your mystical abilities grant you an instinctive knowledge of your surroundings. When you finish a short or long rest, you gain the benefits of [commune with nature](/spells/commune-with-nature-xphb.md).

### Primal Resilience (Level 10)

The mystical power in your blood increases your resiliency in times of need. From 10th level, if you fail a saving throw, you can expend an Adrenaline Surge to add the Constitution modifier of your normal form (a minimum of +1) to your roll, possibly turning a failure into a success.

### Primeval Form (Level 11)

Starting at 11th level, when you Shift into a Bloodline Shape, you can infuse it with primeval power. So long as you remain in that Beast Shape, it gains these additional benefits:

- The Beast takes on a primeval appearance, and grows by one size category, so long as there is room.  
- The Beast gains Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from non-magical attacks.  
- The Beast has advantage on all ability checks and saving throws that use its Strength or Dexterity.  

You can transform into a Primeval Form three times. You regain one expended use when you finish a short rest, and all of your expended uses each time you finish a long rest.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 12)

At 12th level, you can increase one of your ability scores by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You cannot use this feature to increase any ability score above 20.

### Bloodline Feature (Level 13)

You gain a feature from your Bloodline.

### Feral Senses (Level 14)

You hunt as an apex predator, never losing track of your prey. Starting at 14th level, you can't have disadvantage on Natural Weapon attacks against targets within 30 feet of you.

### Mythic Forms (Level 15)

At 15th level, you gain the ability to Shift into truly legendary Beasts of CR 5. However, you can only Shift into each Beast Shape you know of CR 5 and up, once per short or long rest.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 16)

At 16th level, you can increase one of your ability scores by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You cannot use this feature to increase any ability score above 20.

### Primeval Resurgence (Level 17)

In your blood flows a wellspring of primeval power. Starting at 17th level, you can gain the benefits of Primeval Form any time you use Wild Shape to Shift into a Bloodline Shape.

### Bloodline Feature (Level 18)

You gain a feature from your Bloodline.

### Ability Score Improvement (Level 19)

At 19th level, you can increase one of your ability scores by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You cannot use this feature to increase any ability score above 20.

### Force of Nature (Level 20)

Your primeval ferocity cannot be tamed. At 20th level, you can Shift into Beast Shapes with a CR of 5 or 6 without limit.

You also regain one expended use of [Adrenaline Surge](shifter-llshifter.md#Adrenaline%20Surge%20(Level%206)) at the start of each of your turns in combat.
