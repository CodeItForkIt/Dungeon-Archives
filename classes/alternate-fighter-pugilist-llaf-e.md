Most often coming from the school of hard knocks, Fighters known as Pugilists learned to fight in the dark underbelly of society. Where others use tactics and practiced techniques, Pugilists rely on brute force and dirty tricks. To them, every fight is a fight for their life and they find victory at any cost.

## Subclass Features

### Pugilist Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the fighter levels noted in the table below. They don't count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level.

**Pugilist Exploits**

| Fighter Level | Exploits |
|---------------|----------|
| 3rd | [brace up](/optional-features/brace-up-llexploits.md), [streetwise](/optional-features/streetwise-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [concussive blow](/optional-features/concussive-blow-llexploits.md), [dirty hit](/optional-features/dirty-hit-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [disorienting blow](/optional-features/disorienting-blow-llexploits.md) |
^pugilist-exploits

### Contender (Level 3)

Your body itself is a deadly weapon. You learn the [Brawling](/optional-features/brawling-llstyles.md) Fighting Style, and the damage die of your unarmed strikes increases to match the size of your Exploit Die.

If you already know this Fighting Style, you instead learn [Improvised Fighting](/optional-features/improvised-fighting-llstyles.md). Regardless, the Fighting Style you gain here does not count against your number of Fighting Styles.

### Iron Physique (Level 3)

Your battle-hardened physique allows you to shrug off blows that would send a normal person reeling. You can use your Constitution, in place of Dexterity, when calculating your Armor Class in light and medium armor. Moreover, when you are not wearing armor, your Armor Class equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier.

Lastly, whenever you take damage from a source you can see, you can use [brace up](/optional-features/brace-up-llexploits.md) as a reaction, gaining the temporary hit points before you take any damage.

### Counter Punch (Level 7)

You have learned to exploit every opening your foes give you. When a creature you can see misses you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to make an unarmed strike, [Shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md), [Grapple](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md), or improvised weapon attack against it.

If you use force a creature to make a saving throw as part of this reaction attack, it has disadvantage on its roll.

### Resolute Strikes (Level 7)

The grit that only comes from a life on the streets fuels your fists. Your unarmed strikes and improvised weapon attacks count as magical for overcoming resistance and immunity.

### Evasive Footwork (Level 10)

You have trained to evade your foes' strikes in combat. When you take the [Attack](/rules/actions.md#Attack) action on your turn and make at least one unarmed strike, [Grapple](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md), or [Shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md), you also gain the benefits of the [Disengage](/rules/actions.md#Disengage) action until the end of that turn.

### Diamond Physique (Level 15)

You have learned to take hits that would be deadly to weaker warriors. When you use [brace up](/optional-features/brace-up-llexploits.md) with your reaction, you also gain resistance to that instance of damage.

You also learn the [unbreakable](/optional-features/unbreakable-llexploits.md) Exploit, but it doesn't count against your total number of Exploits Known, and you cannot replace it with another Exploit when you gain a level.

### Legendary Pugilist (Level 18)

Your name is known in the underbelly of every city. You have advantage on saving throws to resist and end being [Incapacitated](/rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated), [Paralyzed](/rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed), or [Stunned](/rules/conditions.md#Stunned).

Finally, whenever you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike or improvised weapon, the target falls [prone](/rules/conditions.md#Prone) and is [Stunned](/rules/conditions.md#Stunned) until the start of your next turn.
