### Arcana Domain (Level 2)

You have been chosen by a deity of magic to enact its will upon the world. Infused with an innate knowledge of the arcane arts, you walk the line between mage and priest. Using this divine power, you safeguard arcane secrets and ensure spellcasters do not use their power for evil.

### Divine Arcana (Level 2)

*2nd-level Arcana feature*

Your deity has infused you with a divine knowledge of magic. You gain proficiency in Arcana and you can use your Wisdom, instead of Intelligence, for Arcana checks. If you are already proficient in Arcana, you instead add double your Proficiency Bonus to any Wisdom (Arcana) checks you make.

In addition, you learn two Cantrips of your choice from the Wizard spell list. They count as Cleric spells for you, but they do not count against your number of Cantrips Known. When you gain a Cleric level, you can replace one of these Cantrips with a different Wizard Cantrip of your choice.

### Abjure the Arcane (Level 3)

*3rd-level Arcana feature*

You can draw on the power of your deity to disrupt the magic of spellcasters and arcane entities. As an action, you can hold aloft your holy symbol to Channel Divinity, forcing all Hostile creatures that can cast spells within 30 feet of you to make a Charisma saving throw. If the creature is concentrating on a spell, or is a Celestial, Elemental, Fey, or Fiend, it makes this saving throw with disadvantage.

On a failure, they take force damage equal to 5 times your Cleric level, then are Turned for 1 minute, or until they take damage. A Turned creature can't take reactions, and will use its turns to safely move as far from you as possible, escape an effect preventing movement, or take the Dodge action.

### Spellcraft (Level 3)

Your deity allows you to bend the laws of magic when casting spells. When you cast a Cleric spell, you can Channel Divinity to modify the spell in one of the following ways:

**Elemental Transmutation.** You replace the damage dealt by your spell with acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison damage, and you add your Wisdom modifier to the damage roll.

**Potent Casting.** One target of your choice makes its initial saving throw to resist this spell with disadvantage.

### Purifying Magic (Level 6)

*6th-level Arcana feature*

 Your magic dispels all but the most potent curses and spells. When you restore hit points to a creature with a Cleric spell, you can also end a spell of your choice affecting that creature of a level equal to the spell slot you expended or lower.

### Arcane Archpriest (Level 18)

*18th-level Arcana feature*

Your deity grants you power to rival the greatest archmages. You add one spell of 6th-level, 7th-level, 8th-level, and 9th-level from the Wizard spell list to your Domain Spells, all must be Abjuration, Divination, or Transmutation spells.

Finally, each time you gain a Cleric level, you can replace one of these spells with another Transmutation, Divination, or Abjuration Wizard spell of your choice of the same level.
