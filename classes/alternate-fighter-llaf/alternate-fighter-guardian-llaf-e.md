Guardians are elite defensive warriors whose strengths shine while fighting side by side with their allies. Though trained to use weapons of all types, Guardians are especially effective when using a protective style of fighting with a weapon and shield. Alone, a Guardian is a small threat, but when fighting alongside their allies they are impenetrable walls of steel.

## Subclass Features

### Guardian Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the Fighter levels noted in the table below. They don't count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level.

**Guardian Exploits**

| Fighter Level | Exploits |
|---------------|----------|
| 3rd | [reposition](/optional-features/reposition-llexploits.md), [shield impact](/optional-features/shield-impact-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [defensive stance](/optional-features/defensive-stance-llexploits.md), [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [daring rescue](/optional-features/daring-rescue-llexploits.md) |
^guardian-exploits

### Iron Guardian (Level 3)

You have trained to defend others, no matter the personal cost. You gain one of the following Fighting Styles of your choice: [Defensive Fighting](/optional-features/defensive-fighting-llstyles.md), [Protection](/optional-features/protection-llstyles.md), or [Shield Warrior](/optional-features/shield-warrior-llstyles.md). It doesn't count against your number of Fighting Styles, and can only be replaced by a Style from the list above.

Also, if you are wielding a shield and a creature within 5 feet of you it hit by an attack, you can use [reposition](/optional-features/reposition-llexploits.md) as a reaction. You can choose to do so without expending an Exploit Die, but no temporary hit points are gained.

When you use this reaction, you only take the damage of the triggering attack if it would have also hit you.

### Warrior Smith (Level 3)

You have learned to keep your armor in peak condition. You gain proficiency in [smith's tools](/items/smiths-tools-xphb.md). Whenever you make an ability check that uses smith's tools you gain a bonus to your roll equal to your Exploit Die.

Also, during the course of a long rest, you can spend 1 hour using your smith's tools to improve one set of armor or a shield you touch. It grants its user +1 bonus to Armor Class until the beginning of your next long rest.

### Inspiring Sentinel (Level 7)

You presence inspires others to stand their ground. When you use [Second Wind](alternate-fighter-llaf.md#Second%20Wind%20(Level%201)), creatures of your choice within 5 feet of you gain temporary hit points equal to `1d6` + your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1).

Moreover, when you use your Iron Guardian reaction, [reposition](/optional-features/reposition-llexploits.md) grants temporary hit points even if you do not expend an Exploit Die as part of the reaction.

### Stalwart Defender (Level 10)

You are strongest when standing alongside your allies. You can now use [reposition](/optional-features/reposition-llexploits.md) on creatures within 10 feet of you, including with Iron Guardian, so long as there is a clear path to that creature. Using reposition does not end [defensive stance](/optional-features/defensive-stance-llexploits.md) or [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md) for you.

### Adamant Guardian (Level 15)

You improve your defensive technique to better defend your allies. You can now use [reposition](/optional-features/reposition-llexploits.md) on creatures within 30 feet of you, including with Iron Guardian, so long as there is a clear path for you to move to the creature.

You are also no longer limited to using daring rescue once between each short or long rest.

### Legendary Guardian (Level 18)

You are a legendary shield for those who fight beside you. You gain a special reaction that you can take once per turn, but it can only be used for Iron Guardian, your Guardian Exploits, or [opportunity attacks](/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack).
