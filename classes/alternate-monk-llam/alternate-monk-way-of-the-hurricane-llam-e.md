Where most martial arts traditions focus on quick strikes and quick movement, disciples of the Hurricane technique master mighty weapons. Hurricane Monks wield massive weapons to devastating effect. On the battlefield they become tempests of steel, cutting down any foe who dares to stand against them.

## Subclass Features

### Hurricane Techniques (Level 3)

You learn certain Techniques at the levels noted in the table below. They don't count against your number of Techniques Known and they cannot be switched upon gaining a level.

| Monk Level | Technique |
|------------|-----------|
| 3rd | [typhoon strike](/optional-features/typhoon-strike-llam.md) |
| 5th | [crushing strike](/optional-features/crushing-strike-llam-e.md) |
| 9th | [tremor strike](/optional-features/tremor-strike-llam-e.md) |
^monk-level-technique

Options (choose 1):

- [typhoon strike](/optional-features/typhoon-strike-llam.md)  

### Heavy Warrior (Level 3)

Your style of combat incorporates the mightiest weapons. You gain proficiency with Heavy melee weapons, and your attacks with these weapons count as Martial Arts attacks.

In addition, while you are wielding a Heavy melee weapon, you have advantage on all ability checks and saving throws to resist being Grappled or moved against your will.

### Tempestuous Strike (Level 3)

You can wield your massive weapons with the fury of a great storm. So long as you are wielding a Heavy melee weapon, you can use [Typhoon Strike](/optional-features/typhoon-strike-llam.md) without expending Ki.

When you do so, you can make a single attack with the end of your Heavy weapon as a bonus action on your turn. On hit, it deals damage equal to one Martial Arts attack.

### Signature Techniques (Level 5)

You learn an additional technique for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [crushing strike](/optional-features/crushing-strike-llam-e.md)  

### Crushing Counter (Level 6)

You use the great weight of your massive weapons to rebuke your foes. When a creature you can see hits you with a melee attack while you are wielding a Heavy melee weapon, you can attack it with that Heavy weapon as a reaction.

If you hit with this attack, the force of the blow reduces the attacker's speed to zero until the start of your next turn.

### Signature Techniques (Level 9)

You learn an additional technique for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [tremor strike](/optional-features/tremor-strike-llam-e.md)  

### Buffeting Winds (Level 10)

You empower your strikes with great gusts of wind. When you hit a creature with a Heavy melee weapon attack or damage it with [Typhoon Strike](/optional-features/typhoon-strike-llam.md), you can spend 1 Ki (per target) to force it to make a Strength saving throw against your Technique save DC. On a failure, it is knocked back in a line a number of feet equal to 5 times your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 5 feet).

Also, whenever you use [Typhoon Strike](/optional-features/typhoon-strike-llam.md), you can expend Ki (up to your Wisdom modifier) to empower your Technique. If you do, the Techniques deals bonus thunder damage equal to your Martial Arts Die for each Ki spent. If you do, creatures also take half damage from the Technique on a success.

### Master of the Hurricane (Level 17)

Your skill with massive weapons makes them appear light as a feather. While wielding a Heavy melee weapon you can use your action to disappear and make one melee weapon attack against up to five creatures you can see within 60 feet.

You can use this feature once between each short or long rest, after which, you must spend 5 Ki to use it again.
