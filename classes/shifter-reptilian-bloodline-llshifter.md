Your blood carries the memories of ancient scales and claws. Whether you are the victim of a sinister curse or the chosen scion of a reptilian cult, your bloodline now bears the cold-blooded power of reptilians.

## Subclass Features

### Reptilian Shapes (Level 1)

*1st-level Reptilian Bloodline feature*

Your Bloodline has infused you with an instinctive knowledge of certain animals. Beasts with the *Reptilian* creature subtype are considered Bloodline Shapes for you.

### Reptilian Physiology (Level 1)

The power of your Bloodline grants you certain Reptilian traits when you are not in Beast Shape:

**Natural Armor.** You are covered in a thin sheen of scales; Natural Armor granting you an Armor Class of 10 + your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers.

**Natural Weapons.** Your hands grow elongated claws. They are Natural Weapons with the Light and Finesse properties that deal 1d6 slashing damage on hit.

**Sticky Grip.** You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed, and you can climb up difficult and sheer surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Serpentine Stalker (Level 1)

*1st-level Reptilian Bloodline feature*

You move with the fluidity and silence of a reptilian predator. You gain proficiency in Stealth. If you are already proficient, whenever you make a Stealth check, you treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8.

In addition, when you are in a Bloodline Shape, you can take the Disengage action as a bonus action.

### Adaptive Camouflage (Level 7)

*7th-level Reptilian Bloodline feature*

Your reptilian skin can adapt to blend into any environment. As an action, you can expend one use of [Adrenaline Surge](/classes/shifter-llshifter.md#Adrenaline%20Surge%20(Level%206)) to turn [invisible](/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) for 10 minutes, as if by the [invisibility](/spells/invisibility-xphb.md) spell.

Unlike the spell, this ability does not require [concentration](/rules/conditions.md#Concentration).

### Coiled Strike (Level 7)

You strike at your foes with deadly primeval speed. When you are in a Bloodline Shape, you gain the following benefits:

- You have advantage on opportunity attacks.  
- After a creature you can sense attacks you, you can use your reaction to make an opportunity attack against it.  
- If you have already used your reaction for a round, you can expend an Adrenaline Surge to take another reaction.  

### Crushing Might (Level 13)

*13th-level Reptilian Bloodline feature*

You crush your prey with the primeval force. When a creature starts its turn Grappled or Restrained by you, it must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it takes Bludgeoning damage equal to your level and has disadvantage on all ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws it makes on that turn.

If you are in a Bloodline Shape, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on this Strength saving throw.

### Primeval Predator (Level 18)

*18th-level Reptilian Bloodline feature*

You master the predatory traits evolved by reptiles over the ages. You gain a special Blindsight that allows you to detect heat emitted by anything within 120 feet of you.

Finally, you gain a second reaction that you can use each round. However, you can only use one reaction per trigger.
