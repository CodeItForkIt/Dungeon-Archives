Sometimes called Skalds, Warlords of Gallantry combine the arts of music, strategy, and magic to inspire allies and vex their foes. Learning a unique style of bardic magic that augments their leadership skills, Gallantry Warlords can turn even an unskilled rabble into a deadly fighting force.

## Subclass Features

### Gallant Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the Warlord levels noted in the table below. They do not count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level:

| Warlord Level | Exploit |
|---------------|---------|
| 3rd | [attack order](/optional-features/attack-order-llexploits.md), [steadfast order](/optional-features/steadfast-order-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [enlivening order](/optional-features/enlivening-order-llexploits.md), [heroic will](/optional-features/heroic-will-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [stand the fallen](/optional-features/stand-the-fallen-llexploits.md) |

Options (choose 2):

- [attack order](/optional-features/attack-order-llexploits.md)  
- [steadfast order](/optional-features/steadfast-order-llexploits.md)  

### Gallant Spellcasting (Level 3)

Your talent in poetry and music allows you to produce spells, much like the Bard does. You gain the following benefits:

**Spell Slots.** The Gallant Spellcasting table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st-level and higher. To cast a spell, you must expend a slot of that spell's level or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest. For example, if you know the [thunderwave](/spells/thunderwave-xphb.md) spell and have both a 1st and a 2nd-level spell slot, you can cast thunderwave using either spell slot.

**Spells Known of 1st-Level and Higher.** You know three 1st-level spells of your choice from the Gallantry spell list on the following page. The Spells Known column of the Gallant Spellcasting table shows when you learn more spells from this list of 1st-level and up. Any spells you learn must be of a level for which you have spell slots as shown on the table.

When you gain a level, you can replace one of your Spells Known with another spell from the Galantry spell list. The spell must also be of a level for which you have spell slots.

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use any musical instrument you are proficient with as a spellcasting focus for your spells. Gallantry Warlords are known for one-handed instruments, like war horns, while holding a weapon in their other hand.

**Spellcasting Ability.** Use your Leadership modifier when setting the saving throw DC or making a spell attack roll for any Gallantry spells you know through this feature:

<span class='abilityDc'>**Spell save DC**: your Leadership modifier + Proficiency Bonus</span>

<span class='abilityAttackMod'>**Spell attack modifier**: your Leadership modifier + Proficiency Bonus</span>

### Warrior Poet (Level 3)

You combine martial skill and musical talent to inspire your allies. You gain proficiency with two musical instruments of your choice, and in [Performance](/rules/skills.md#Performance).

Moreover, whenever you make a Performance check that includes both a weapon and a musical instrument, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to your Warlord level.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [enlivening order](/optional-features/enlivening-order-llexploits.md)  
- [heroic will](/optional-features/heroic-will-llexploits.md)  

### Heroic Charge (Level 6)

When you roll initiative, you can expend a use of Inspiring Word to rally a heroic charge. You, and any creatures of your choice within 15 feet that can hear you gain the benefits of the [Dash](/rules/actions.md#Dash) action during your first turn in combat. You cannot use this feature if you are [surprised](/rules/conditions.md#Surprised) or [Incapacitated](/rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

At 11th level, the range of this feature increases to 30 feet.

### Songs of War & Peace (Level 6)

You conduct battle like a great symphony. When you expend a spell slot or Exploit Die as part of your action, you can make a single weapon attack as a bonus action on that turn.

In addition, any creature that spends a short rest with you has advantage on Hit Die rolls to regain its hit points.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [stand the fallen](/optional-features/stand-the-fallen-llexploits.md)  

### Warsong (Level 14)

The Skalds of legend were said to be able to turn the tide of the most desperate battles. Whenever you target a creature with Inspiring Word or a Tactical Exploit, it has advantage on the first ability check, attack roll, or saving throw it makes before the beginning of your next turn.

It also can't be [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [Frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) for that duration.

### Mythic Voice (Level 18)

Your voice itself has been infused with the powerful magic you channel. Whenever you cast a 1st-level Gallantry spell, you can choose to expend a use of Inspiring Word instead of a spell slot. You must use a musical instrument as the spellcasting focus for the spell.
