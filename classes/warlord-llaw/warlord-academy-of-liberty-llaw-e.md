Often forced into positions of leadership, Warlords of Liberty come from the ranks of the common folk. When a dark tyrant begins to oppress the weak, when a monarch raises taxes, or when a village is conquered by invaders, leaders will arise to lead their peoples' resistance against injustice.

## Subclass Features

### Coordinated Assault (Level 3)

While individually you may not be a threat, together you and your comrades can triumph over any enemy. You can issue a [support order](/optional-features/support-order-llexploits.md) as a bonus action on each of your turns.

### Liberty Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the Warlord levels noted in the table below. They do not count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level:

| Warlord Level | Exploit |
|---------------|---------|
| 3rd | [first aid](/optional-features/first-aid-llexploits.md), [support order](/optional-features/support-order-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [rejuvenating order](/optional-features/rejuvenating-order-llexploits.md), [surprise attack](/optional-features/surprise-attack-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [inspirational speech](/optional-features/inspirational-speech-llexploits.md) |
^warlord-level-exploit

Options (choose 2):

- [first aid](/optional-features/first-aid-llexploits.md)  
- [support order](/optional-features/support-order-llexploits.md)  

### Salt of the Earth (Level 3)

You hail from the ranks of the common folk, and a life of hard work has made you tough. Your hit point maximum increases by 3, and increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.

Also, whenever you make an ability check to interact with common people, such as farmers, laborers, or foot soldiers, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to your Exploit Die.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [rejuvenating order](/optional-features/rejuvenating-order-llexploits.md)  
- [surprise attack](/optional-features/surprise-attack-llexploits.md)  

### Band Together (Level 6)

Separated you are weak, but together you are strong! When a creature within 15 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to rally allies to their side. You divide the damage the creature would take evenly among willing creatures within 15 feet of it. All creatures must be able to hear you to participate.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [inspirational speech](/optional-features/inspirational-speech-llexploits.md)  

### Strength in Numbers (Level 14)

The more comrades you rally to your cause, the greater your leadership potential. When you roll for initiative, you regain a number of your expended Exploit Dice equal to the number of allied creatures within 15 feet of you.

You also learn the [equip militia](/optional-features/equip-militia-llexploits.md) Exploit. It does not count against your Exploits Known, and it cannot be replaced. You can use equip milita during a short or long rest, and once per long rest, you can use it without expending an Exploit Die.

Options (choose 1):

- [equip militia](/optional-features/equip-militia-llexploits.md)  

### Grand Revolutionary (Level 18)

You are destined to upset the status quo wherever you take up the mantle of leadership. The range of Band Together and Strength in Numbers includes creatures within 30 feet.

Finally, whenever you use Inspiring Word or Rallying Cry, you can target up to three creatures within range, instead of one. For Rallying Cry, all creatures must have failed the same saving throw to be targeted by the same Rallying Cry.
