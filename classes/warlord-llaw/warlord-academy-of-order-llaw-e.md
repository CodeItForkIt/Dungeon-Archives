Civilization thrives within the bounds of lawful society, and you are a defender of that principle. You employ your talent for leadership in the upholding of law and the enforcement of order. You are a thorn in the side of villians, and a shield for all those who live within the bounds of just laws.

## Subclass Features

### Order Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the Warlord levels noted in the table below. They do not count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level:

| Warlord Level | Exploit |
|---------------|---------|
| 3rd | [defensive order](/optional-features/defensive-order-llexploits.md), [disarm](/optional-features/disarm-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md), [soothing speech](/optional-features/soothing-speech-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [survey settlement](/optional-features/survey-settlement-llexploits.md) |

Options (choose 2):

- [disarm](/optional-features/disarm-llexploits.md)  

### Keeper of Order (Level 3)

You employ your tactical training to uphold law and order in civilized society. You gain proficiency in either [Insight](/rules/skills.md#Insight) or [Investigation](/rules/skills.md#Investigation), and whenever you make an ability check with either of these skills, you can choose to use your Leadership Ability for the check.

In addition, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a non-lethal melee attack, you can use a bonus action on that turn to restrain them with a [net](/items/net-xphb.md), rope, [manacles](/items/manacles-xphb.md), or another similar object. The DC to escape this restraint is equal to your Exploit save DC unless its innate escape DC was already higher.

### Law's Shield (Level 3)

It is your duty to uphold the law and defend the weak. When a creature you can see within 15 feet is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to issue it a [defensive order](/optional-features/defensive-order-llexploits.md), imposing disadvantage on the triggering attack, possibly turning a hit into a miss.

You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your Leadership modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. When you have no uses, you can expend an Inspiring Word to use it again.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md)  
- [soothing speech](/optional-features/soothing-speech-llexploits.md)  

### Halt (Level 6)

Your words carry lawful authority. When a creature you can see within 30 feet moves, you can use your reaction to spend a use of Inspiring Word and force it to make a Wisdom saving throw. The target must be able to hear and understand you.

On a failed save, its speed is reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. Once a creature succeeds on this saving throw, it is immune to this feature for the next 24 hours.

### Stalwart Defender (Level 6)

You are the shield that holds back the forces of lawlessness and chaos. Once per short or long rest, you can use [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md) without expending an Exploit Die.

When using hold the line, its range is 15 feet, and you can issue a [defensive order](/optional-features/defensive-order-llexploits.md) as a bonus action on each turn.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [survey settlement](/optional-features/survey-settlement-llexploits.md)  

### Bastion of Order (Level 14)

You are an unwavering shield for those who live within the bounds of the law. While you are using [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md), you can use your Law's Shield and Halt reactions without expending uses of either feature or Inspiring Word.

Moreover, you can move up to 10 feet on your turn without ending the effects of hold the line.

### Unerring Eye (Level 14)

Your keen sight pierces all deceptions. You can automatically detect the presence of Illusion magic and Shapeshifters, but you are not able to see the true forms that they disguise.

### High Authority (Level 18)

You are the highest authority of the law, only superseded by monarchs and divine agents. You always exude the effects of [hold the line](/optional-features/hold-the-line-llexploits.md) to creatures of your choice within 15 feet of you, but you can move and act as normal.

Finally, when a creature fails its saving throw against Halt, it is also [Restrained](/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) until the start of its next turn.
