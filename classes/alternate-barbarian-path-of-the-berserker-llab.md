The savage warriors known as berserkers live for the chaos of battle. For these barbarians, their Rage is a manifestation of their lust for violence and destruction. Often found on the front lines of battle, berserkers only feel fully alive when they are risking life and limb to test their might against their foes.

## Subclass Features

### Berserker Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the barbarian levels noted in the table below. These don't count against your number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level.

| Barbarian Level | Exploit |
|-----------------|---------|
| 3rd | [imposing presence](/optional-features/imposing-presence-llexploits.md), [savage rebuke](/optional-features/savage-rebuke-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [bloodthirsty critical](/optional-features/bloodthirsty-critical-llexploits.md), [menacing shout](/optional-features/menacing-shout-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [roar of triumph](/optional-features/roar-of-triumph-llexploits.md) |

### Frenzy (Level 3)

Your fury in battle surpasses even other Barbarians. A truly terrifying sight to behold on the battlefield. The first time on each of your turns that you hit a creature with a Reckless Attack, you deal bonus damage equal to twice your Exploit Die.

Your lust for the chaos and blood of battle propels you. While Raging, your speed increases by 10 feet.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [bloodthirsty critical](/optional-features/bloodthirsty-critical-llexploits.md)  
- [menacing shout](/optional-features/menacing-shout-llexploits.md)  

### Mindless Rage (Level 6)

Your Rage has grown so intense that you lose yourself in its primal grip. You can't be Charmed or Frightened while Raging. If you are Charmed or Frightened when you Rage, that effect is suspended for the duration.

Moreover, while Raging, you can ignore the effects of the first five levels of Exhaustion that you have.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [roar of triumph](/optional-features/roar-of-triumph-llexploits.md)  

### Intimidating Presence (Level 10)

The unchecked ferocity that dwells within you inspires primal fear in your enemies. You can use the menacing shout Exploit at-will, without expending an Exploit Die a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (a minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses of this feature when you finish a long rest.

In addition, if you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, it has disadvantage on any saving throws you force it to make to resist being Frightened until the end of your following turn.

### Unrivaled Fury (Level 14)

When you Rage, you are no longer yourself, giving in to the whirlwind of primeval violence that rages inside you. You can use the savage rebuke Exploit at will, without expending an Exploit Die.

You can also apply your Frenzy bonus damage once during each turn in combat, not just on your turn.
