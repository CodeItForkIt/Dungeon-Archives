### Trickery Domain (Level 2)

You have been deemed worthy to wield the divine magick of a deity of Trickery. Often patrons of thieves and pranksters, you have been given power by a deity to sow chaos in their name.

### Trickster's Mantle (Level 2)

*2nd-level Trickery feature*

You can cloak yourself and others in the divine deceptions of your deity. As an action, you can touch a creature, including yourself and grant it proficiency in Stealth. If already proficient in Stealth, they can add double their Proficiency Bonus to any Stealth checks they make.

This effect lasts for the target until you use this feature on another creature, you choose to end it, you finish a long rest, or you are slain.

### Illusory Duplicate (Level 3)

*3rd-level Trickery feature*

As an action, you can grasp your Holy Symbol and Channel Divinity to conjure your Illusory Duplicate, a perfect copy of yourself in an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet. The Illusory Duplicate lasts for 1 minute, but it disappears if you become Incapacitated, you are more than 120 feet from it, or you dismiss it (no action needed). The Illusory Duplicate uses the following rules:

**Appearance.** Your Duplicate is a perfect copy of you. It mimics your expressions and gestures, but it is incorporeal, cannot affect objects, and does not occupy its space.

**Movement.** As a bonus action, you can have your Illusory Duplicate move up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space within 120 feet of you.

**Spellcasting.** You can cast Cleric spells as if you were in your Illusory Duplicate's space, but you must use your own senses.

**Trickery.** If both you and your Duplicate are within 5 feet of a creature, you have advantage on your attack rolls against that creature.

### Mischievous Mask (Level 3)

*3rd-level Trickery feature*

As an action, you touch your Holy Symbol to yourself or another creature and Channel Divinity to cast the [disguise self](/spells/disguise-self-xphb.md) spell on it. Unwilling targets must make a Constitution saving throw or suffer its effects.

### Tricky Transposition (Level 6)

*6th-level Trickery feature*

 The magic of your Illusory Duplicate has increased in service of your deity. Any time that you conjure, or move your Illusory Duplicate, you can undetectably switch places with it.

### Perfect Prank (Level 18)

*18th-level Trickery feature*

 You can unleash magic stored within an Illusory Duplicate to vex your foes and aid your allies. When you cast a Cleric spell that targets a creature within 5 feet of your Illusory Duplicate you can dismiss the Duplicate to cause one of these effects:

**Debilitating Distraction.** The creature must subtract your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1) from its initial saving throw to resist the effects of your spell.

**Healing Hoax.** The creature regains (additional) hit points equal to your Cleric level.
