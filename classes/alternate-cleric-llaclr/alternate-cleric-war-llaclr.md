### War Domain (Level 2)

Deities of all alignments in the great cosmic struggle bestow the War Domain on their Clerics. Some charge their soldiers to stand in defense of the innocent, while others demand they use this divine power to conquer and subdue. Some Clerics of War become great heroes, and others feared warlords.

### Celestial Armory (Level 2)

*2nd-level War feature*

You can channel the power of your deity of War through your weapons. For you, any melee weapon you are proficient with counts as a Holy Symbol for your Cleric spells and features.

You can also call down power from your deity's armory. You can cast the [divine favor (alternate)](/spells/divine-favor-alternate-llspell.md) or the [shield of faith](/spells/shield-of-faith-xphb.md) spell at 1st-level, targeting yourself, without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all uses when you complete a long rest.

### Divine Strike (Level 3)

*3rd-level War feature*

You infuse your strikes with the divine intent of a War deity. When you miss with a Consecrated Weapon attack, you can Channel Divinity to add your Cleric level to your attack roll, possibly turning your missed attack into a hit.

Beginning at 6th level, you can use this Channel Divinity as a reaction when a creature you can see within 30 feet misses with a weapon attack, granting it the same bonus to its roll.

### Blessed Fortitude (Level 3)

*3rd-level War feature*

 You can fortify your magic with the divine power of your deity. When you cast a Domain Spell which requires concentration, you can Channel Divinity to fortify that spell. For its duration, your concentration cannot be broken by taking damage.

### War Magic (Level 6)

*6th-level War feature*

Your devotion to warfare allows you to weave spell and steel together. When you use your action to Cast a Cleric Spell, you can make one melee weapon attack as a bonus action.

### Paragon of War (Level 18)

*18th-level War feature*

You are marked as a divine champion of war itself. Whenever you take the Attack action, you can make two attacks instead of one. Moreover, when you use your action to take the Dash or Disengage action, you can make one melee weapon attack as a bonus action on that turn.

Finally, you gain Resistance to all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from weapon attacks.
