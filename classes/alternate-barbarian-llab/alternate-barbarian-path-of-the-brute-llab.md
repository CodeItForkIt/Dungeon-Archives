While most barbarians hail from the wild places of the world, brutes discovered their Rage in the underbelly of civilization. Their abilities stem from a combination of street smarts and pure grit. All those who follow this Path share one thing in common, they prefer to solve their problems with their fists.

## Subclass Features

### Brutish Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the barbarian levels noted in the table below. These don't count against your number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level.

| Barbarian Level | Exploit |
|-----------------|---------|
| 3rd | [imposing presence](/optional-features/imposing-presence-llexploits.md), [crushing grip](/optional-features/crushing-grip-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [concussive blow](/optional-features/concussive-blow-llexploits.md), [mighty hurl](/optional-features/mighty-hurl-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [confounding critical](/optional-features/confounding-critical-llexploits.md) |

- [crushing grip](/optional-features/crushing-grip-llexploits.md)  

### The Wrong Crowd (Level 3)

You always seem to find yourself with the roughest crowd in any town you find yourself in. You can speak, decipher, and leave secret messages in Thieves' Cant.

In addition, spending time Carousing counts as resting for you, so long as you don't deal or take any damage. After you spend a night Carousing in a settlement, you have advantage on ability checks to gather information about the settlement.

### Unarmed & Dangerous (Level 3)

 Your life in the underbelly of civilization has forged your body into a deadly weapon. You gain the benefits listed below:

- You can use your Constitution, instead of Dexterity, when calculating your Armor Class in light and medium armor.  
- You gain proficiency with improvised weapons.  
- Your damage die for improvised weapons and unarmed strikes equals your Exploit Die unless it was higher.  
- When you take the Attack action while Raging and only make unarmed strikes, Grapple, Shove, or improvised weapon attacks, you can make another one of the attacks from the list above as a bonus action on that turn.  

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [concussive blow](/optional-features/concussive-blow-llexploits.md)  
- [mighty hurl](/optional-features/mighty-hurl-llexploits.md)  

### Brutal Strikes (Level 6)

Fury empowers your strikes. While Raging, your unarmed strikes and improvised weapon attacks count as magical.

Also, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can use [concussive blow](/optional-features/concussive-blow-llexploits.md) without expending an Exploit Die. You can do so a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier. You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [confounding critical](/optional-features/confounding-critical-llexploits.md)  

### Iron Grip (Level 10)

 When Raging, you can Grapple targets up to two sizes larger than you, your speed isn't halved while Grappling, and when you make an Athletics check to Grapple or Shove, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to your Exploit Die.

### Brutish Determination (Level 14)

You can shrug off physical blows that would devastate lesser warriors. When you make a Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, or death saving throw, you roll a d4 and add it to your roll.

Should you roll a 20 or higher on a death saving throw, you instantly regain consciousness and stand up with 1 hit point.
