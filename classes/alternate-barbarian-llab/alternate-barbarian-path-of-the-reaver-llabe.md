While most barbarians focus on overcoming their foes with raw power and brute force, those known as Reavers augment their impressive strength with martial technique and tactical maneuvers. These fearless warriors are often employed as advance forces, and their combat skill and resilience strikes fear into the heart of any who stand against them in battle.

## Subclass Features

### Reaver Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the barbarian levels noted in the table below. These don't count against your number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level.

| Barbarian Level | Exploit |
|-----------------|---------|
| 3rd | [savage rebuke](/optional-features/savage-rebuke-llexploits.md), [taunting strike](/optional-features/taunting-strike-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [glancing blow](/optional-features/glancing-blow-llexploits.md), [rending strike](/optional-features/rending-strike-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [adrenaline rush](/optional-features/adrenaline-rush-llexploits.md) |
^barbarian-level-exploit

Options (choose 2):

- [taunting strike](/optional-features/taunting-strike-llexploits.md)  
- [savage rebuke](/optional-features/savage-rebuke-llexploits.md)  

### Reaver Superiority (Level 3)

You have studied martial techniques to enhance your skill in battle. Your total number of Exploit Dice increases by 1, and you learn one additional Exploit of your choice which doesn't count against your total number of Exploits Known.

Moreover, when you learn a new Exploit, you can choose to learn a Savage Exploit from those available to this class, or one of the [Martial Exploits](/lists/list-optfeaturetype-ll-me.md) from those available to the Alternate Fighter.

If you choose to learn a Martial Exploit that has a certain fighter level prerequisite, you can learn that Exploit if your barbarian level would meet that prerequisite level.

### Swift Strides (Level 3)

Rather than become blinded by Rage, you enter a heightened state of focus and move with purpose. When you are Raging, opportunity attacks against you are made at disadvantage.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [glancing blow](/optional-features/glancing-blow-llexploits.md)  
- [rending strike](/optional-features/rending-strike-llexploits.md)  

### Restorative Rage (Level 6)

Your combat prowess rivals that of heroes of great renown. Whenever you Rage, you gain one of the following benefits:

- You regain one of your expended Exploit Dice.   
- You gain temporary hit points equal to your Exploit Die.   
- Your speed is doubled for your current turn.  

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [adrenaline rush](/optional-features/adrenaline-rush-llexploits.md)  

### Unstoppable Warrior (Level 10)

The chaos and danger of battle fuels your battle focus. While Raging, you are under the effects of the [freedom of movement](/spells/freedom-of-movement-xphb.md) spell, but this is not considered a magical effect.

You also learn one additional Exploit of your choice which does not count against your total number of Exploits Known. As normal, you can choose this Exploit from the Barbarian or the Fighter class Exploit list.

### Storm of Steel (Level 14)

You have reached the pinnacle of your training, and woe to those who stand against you on the field of battle. One per turn when you hit with a weapon attack while Raging, you can forgo the Rage damage bonus for that attack to instead use an Exploit you know without expending an Exploit Die. The Exploit you use in this way must be able to be used as part of a weapon attack.

For example, if you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you could forgo the normal damage bonus to use rending strike without expending an Exploit Die.
