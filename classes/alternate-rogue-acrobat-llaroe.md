Acrobats are acrobatic Rogues who seek danger out, living for the adrenaline rush of near-death experiences. Acrobats would rather leap across rooftops than walk the streets, and in turn, they develop a knack for avoiding certain death.

## Subclass Features

### Acrobat Exploits (Level 3)

You learn certain Exploits at the rogue levels noted in the table below. These don't count against your total number of Exploits Known and can't be switched upon gaining a level.

| Rogue Level | Exploit |
|-------------|---------|
| 3rd | [aerial maneuver](/optional-features/aerial-maneuver-llexploits.md), [lunge](/optional-features/lunge-llexploits.md) |
| 5th | [dirty hit](/optional-features/dirty-hit-llexploits.md), [trick shot](/optional-features/trick-shot-llexploits.md) |
| 9th | [survey settlement](/optional-features/survey-settlement-llexploits.md) |

Options (choose 2):

- [lunge](/optional-features/lunge-llexploits.md)  

### Acrobatic Training (Level 3)

 You have honed your body into the perfect tool, able to strike and maneuver with precision. You gain the following benefits:

- You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.  
- You can climb difficult and sheer surfaces without making an ability check if you move at half your normal speed.  
- You can use Dexterity to calculate your jump distances.  
- Your unarmed strikes gain the finesse property, and on hit, they deal bludgeoning damage equal to your Exploit Die + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice).  

### Flying Strike (Level 3)

When you fall at least 5 feet then hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, your attack qualifies for Sneak Attack, so long as you do not have disadvantage on your attack roll.

Your target must then succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Exploit save DC or it is knocked Prone. Targets larger than you have advantage on this saving throw.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 5)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 2):

- [dirty hit](/optional-features/dirty-hit-llexploits.md)  
- [trick shot](/optional-features/trick-shot-llexploits.md)  

### Death from Above (Level 7)

 You strike at your foes from unorthodox airborne positions. If you make a weapon attack while airborne and at least 10 feet off the ground you have advantage on your attack roll.

### Slow Fall (Level 7)

Your experience falling from great heights has made you an expert at landing without hurting yourself. You can use [aerial maneuver](/optional-features/aerial-maneuver-llexploits.md) at-will, without expending an Exploit Die.

If you land on something soft to breaks your fall, like a body of water or wagon of hay, you take no damage from that fall.

### Archetype Exploits (Level 9)

You learn additional exploits for your archetype.

Options (choose 1):

- [survey settlement](/optional-features/survey-settlement-llexploits.md)  

### Defy Death (Level 13)

 Your many close brushes with death have made you adept at escaping it. You add your Proficiency Bonus to death saving throws. When you make a death saving throw and roll above a 20, it has the same effect as if you rolled a 20 on the d20.

### Stunning Leap (Level 13)

 As a bonus action, you can spend 5 feet of movement to jump up to 20 feet, even if this would exceed your remaining speed.

### Masterful Aerialist (Level 17)

 You are unparalleled in your ability to maneuver your body. Whenever you make a Dexterity ability check or saving throw and the total result is lower than your Dexterity score, you can use your Dexterity score in place of your roll.
