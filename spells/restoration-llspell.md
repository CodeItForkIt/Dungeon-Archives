# Restoration
*2nd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a willing creature and instantly end one disease or one of the following conditions currently afflicting it: [blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded), [deafened](/rules/conditions.md#Deafened), [frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened), [paralyzed](/rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed), or [poisoned](/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned).

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd-level or higher, the conditions and effects you can end increase, but the spell requires you to expend diamond dust of a certain value, which is consumed during casting:

**Restoration**

| Slot Level | Value | Conditions/Effects |
|------------|-------|--------------------|
| 3rd | 10 gp | [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed), [Exhaustion](/rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) (1 level) |
| 4th | 50 gp | Reduction to one Ability Score or Hit Point Maximum |
| 5th | 100 gp | [Petrified](/rules/conditions.md#Petrified), One Curse, or Attunement to a Cursed Item |
^restoration
