# Shield (Alternate)
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction, which you take when you are hit by an attack you can see or targeted by [magic missile](/spells/magic-missile-xphb.md)
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A shimmering barrier of magical force appears to protect you. Until the end of the current turn, you gain a bonus to your Armor Class equal to 1 + your Spellcasting modifier and you take no damage from the [magic missile](/spells/magic-missile-xphb.md) spell.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd-level or higher, the bonus to your Armor Class increases by 1 for each slot level above 1st.
