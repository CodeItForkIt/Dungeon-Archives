# Glitterbeam
*cantrip, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You project a beam of twinkling lights at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes `1d8` radiant damage. Until a creature uses an action to remove the glitter, a target hit by this spell emits bright light in a 5-foot radius and has disadvantage on any Dexterity ([Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks it makes to hide or conceal itself.

**At Higher Levels.** The damage of this spell increases by 1d8 at 5th (`2d8`), 11th (`3d8`), and 17th level (`4d8`).
