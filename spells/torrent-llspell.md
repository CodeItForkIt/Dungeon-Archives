# Torrent
*1st-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Line)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a mirror)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A burst of elemental water erupts from you in a line 30 feet long and 5 feet wide in a direction you choose, forcing any creature in that area to make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it takes `1d12` cold damage and is knocked back 10 feet in a straight line and falls [prone](/rules/conditions.md#Prone). On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not moved or knocked prone. A Huge or larger creature has advantage on its saving throw.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd-level or higher, the damage increases by `1d12` and it knocks back 10 additional feet for each slot level above 1st.
