# True Polymorph (Alternate)
*9th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of mercury, a dollop of gum arabic, and a wisp of smoke)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You point your finger at one creature or non-magical object you can see in range, transforming it in one of the ways listed below. An unwilling target makes a Charisma saving throw to resist this effect, and Shapechangers automatically succeed on this saving throw. This spell cannot affect a target with 0 hit points, or an object that is being worn or carried.

If you concentrate on the effect for the full duration of this spell, the transformation becomes permanent for your target.

## Creature to Creature

The creature is transformed into another creature of a CR equal to (or lower then) half its CR or level. You must have seen a living specimen of the creature before in order to transform the target into that creature. The targets's game statistics are replaced by those of the creature, only retaining its alignment, personality, and _Hit Points_. All of its gear merges with its new form, and it cannot activate, use, or benefit from it while transformed. It can only take actions available to its new form, including speech, spellcasting, and things that require specific anatomy, such as hands.

## Creature to Object

The creature is transformed into a non-magical object equal to its size or smaller. Everything it is wearing or carrying is transformed with it. You must have seen this object before to transform the creature into it. The target's statistics are replaced with those of the object. If the transformation ends, the target has no memory of the time it spent as the object.

## Object to Creature

The non-magical object transforms into a creature equal to the objects size (or smaller) of CR 9 or lower. You must have seen a live specimen of the creature before in order to transform the object into that creature. Its statistics are replaced by those of the creature, it is Friendly to you and your allies, it rolls its own initiative in combat, and you decide how it acts. Should the spell becomes permanent the new creature becomes independent, and it decides how to relate to you based how it was treated under your control.

## Object ot Object

The non-magical object is transformed into another non-magical object you have seen before, of its size or smaller. It statistics are replaced by the new object.
