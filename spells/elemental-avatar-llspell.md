# Elemental Avatar
*6th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You draw upon the chaotic energies of the elemental planes, infusing yourself with pure elemental Air, Earth, Fire, or Ice. For the duration, you gain the benefits of that element:

## Air

A vortex of wind swirls about you, causing all ranged weapon attacks targeting you to be made with disadvantage, and granting you a flying speed equal to your walking speed.

As an action, you can unleash a tempestuous burst, forcing creatures within 15 feet to make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, they take `4d8` thunder damage and are pushed away from you 15 feet in a straight line. On a successful save, they take half as much damage and are not moved.

## Earth

Your body is covered in a shifting layer of raw earth and stone, granting you Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. You can also ignore any difficult terrain imposed by earth, stone, sand, or similar materials.

As an action, you rend the earth at your feet out to a 15-foot radius turning it into difficult terrain and forcing all creatures in that area to make a Dexterity saving throw. They take `4d8` bludgeoning damage and fall [Prone](/rules/conditions.md#Prone) on a failed save, and half as much damage and do not fall Prone on a success.

## Fire

Flames envelop your body, harmless to you. You gain Immunity to fire damage, Resistance to radiant damage, and you shed bright light out to a 30-foot radius.

As an action, you unleash a burst of searing fire, forcing all creatures within 15 feet to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take `4d8` fire damage and are [Blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded) until the start of your next turn. On a success, they take half as much damage and are not Blinded.

## Ice

A thin sheen of glacial frost covers your body. You gain Immunity to cold damage, and the area within 15 feet of you becomes difficult terrain for creatures other than you due to the frigid temperature emitting from your body.

As an action, you can flash freeze the area within 15 feet, forcing creatures to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they take `4d8` cold damage and their speed is halved until the start of your next turn. On a success, they take half as much damage and their speed is not affected.

## At Higher Levels

If you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th-level or higher, you gain the benefits of another element of your choice (you can only choose each element once), and the damage of the special actions granted by this spell increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 5th.
