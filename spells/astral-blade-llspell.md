# Astral Blade
*6th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You conjure a 3-foot blade of pure astral energy. It appears in a space you can see within range, and you instantly make two melee spell attacks with it against targets within 5 feet. Astral Blade attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 18-20 on the d20. On hit, they deal `4d6` force damage. On a critical hit, its target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be banished to a harmless Demiplane until the start of your next turn.

On subsequent turns, you can use a bonus action to move your Astral Blade up to 60 feet and make two attacks with it.

Your Astral Blade can harmlessly pass through any barrier created with magic, like those of a [wall of force](/spells/wall-of-force-alternate-llspell.md) spell.

**At Higher Levels.** If you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th-level or higher, the force damage dealt by the Astral Blade increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 6th.
