# Speak with Dead (Alternate)
*3rd-level, Necromancy (ritual)*  


- **Casting time:** 10 minutes unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (two gold coins, consumed by the spell)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You grant a semblance of life and intelligence to a Humanoid corpse of your choice within range, allowing it to answer your questions. It must still have a mouth and it cannot be Undead. If this corpse was the target of another speak with dead spell in the past 10 days, this casting of the spell fails.

While the spell lasts, the spellcaster can ask the corpse up to three questions. Any answers requested beyond a "yes" or "no" are brief and cryptic. The corpse is not compelled to give truthful answers, especially if the spellcaster was Hostile to it or it recognizes the spellcaster as an enemy.

This spell does not return the soul to the corpse's body, but draws out the imprint of its consciousness from the moments before its death. The corpse cannot learn new information, it cannot comprehend anything that has happened since it died, and it cannot speculate about the future.
