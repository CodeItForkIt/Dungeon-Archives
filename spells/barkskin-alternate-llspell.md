# Barkskin (Alternate)
*2nd-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a strip of oak bark)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature that is not wearing metal armor. For the duration, it's skin has a rough, bark-like appearance, and its Armor Class cannot be lower than 16. The spell ends if the target is [Incapacitated](/rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated) or dons metal armor, but it can wield a shield and retain this benefit.
