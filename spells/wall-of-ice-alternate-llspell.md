# Wall of Ice (Alternate)
*4th-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a small piece of quartz)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a 1-foot thick wall of ice on a solid surface within range. You create it as a hemispherical dome or a sphere with radius of up to 10 feet, or you shape a flat surface made up of ten 10-foot-square panels. All panels must be contiguous with another panel. The ice wall lasts for the duration.

When the wall appears, creatures in its space must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, you decide which side of the wall they are pushed to, then they take `6d6` cold damage. On a success, they decide which side they are pushed to, and they take half as much cold damage.

The wall has an AC of 12, 30 hit points per 10-foot section, and it is Vulnerable to fire damage. When a 10-foot section is destroyed, a sheet of frigid air remains in that space. When a creature passes through a sheet of this frigid air it must make a Constitution saving throw. It takes `2d6` cold damage on a failure, and half as much on a success. A creature can only be forced to make this saving throw once per turn.

**At Higher Levels.** If you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th-level or higher, the damage the wall deals when it appears increases by `2d6`, and damage from passing through a sheet of frigid air increases by `1d6`, for each slot level above 4th.
