# Sword Burst (Alternate)
*cantrip, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** Special ([1 attack](/rules/variant-rules/casting-time-1-attack-llspell.md))
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a defensive ring of spectral blades which sweep around you. Creatures within 5 feet of you must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take `1d6` slashing damage.

**At Higher Levels.** The damage die of this spell increases at 5th level (`1d8`), 11th level (`1d10`), and 17th level (`1d12`).

**Classes**: [Alternate Sorcerer (Elder Sorcery)](/lists/list-spells-classes-elder-sorcery-llasorcorc-e.md "subclass=LLASorcorc:E;class=LLASorc"); [Warlock](/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md)
