# Psionic Oppression
*6th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You psionically overwhelm the mind of one creature you can see within range. It must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw or it takes `12d6` psychic damage and is [stunned](/rules/conditions.md#Stunned) for 1 minute. On a successful save, the target takes half as much psychic damage and is not stunned.

The stunned target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
