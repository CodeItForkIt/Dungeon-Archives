# Dominate Creature
*4th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You attempt to beguile a Beast that you can see within range. It must make a Wisdom saving throw or become [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed) by you for the duration. If you or creatures that are Friendly to you are fighting it, it has advantage on its saving throw.

While Charmed, you share a telepathic link with the Beast so long as you are on the same plane. You can issue orders to the creature through this link, which it does its best to follow, so long as you are not [Incapacitated](/rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated). You can give specific or general orders, such as "attack that creature" or "protect that door". When the creature does not have an order to follow, it will defend itself to the best of its abilities.

As an action, you can take complete control of the creature until the start of your next turn. When you do so, it takes only the actions you choose and nothing beyond it. You can cause it to use a reaction, but you must use your reaction as well.

Any time the creature takes damage, it repeats its Wisdom saving throw, ending this effect on a successful save.

**At Higher Levels.** As you cast this spell with a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the spell's effects increase, as shown here:

| Slot Level | Increased Effect |
|------------|------------------|
| 5th | The spell can target Humanoids |
| 6th | Duration increases to 10 minutes |
| 7th | Duration increases to 1 hour |
| 8th | The spell can target any creature |
| 9th | Duration increases to 8 hours |
