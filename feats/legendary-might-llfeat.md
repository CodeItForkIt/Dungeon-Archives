# Legendary Might
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, Alternate Barbarian Expanded*  

You have always found ways to leverage your great might for additional benefits. You gain the benefits on the table below that correspond to your Strength modifier and lower.

Should your Strength modifier increase, the benefits that you gain from this Feat also increase with your modifier:

| Modifier | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| +1 | You count as one size larger for the purpose of carrying capacity, the size of creatures you can [Grapple](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md), and the weight you can push, pull, lift, or drag at one time. |
| +2 | You can make Strength ([Intimidation](/rules/skills.md#Intimidation)) checks in place of Charisma ([Intimidation](/rules/skills.md#Intimidation)) checks. |
| +3 | You gain a climbing speed or a swimming speed equal to your walking speed. |
| +4 | Whenever you make an [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics) check, you treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8. |
| +5 | When you are forced to make a Constitution saving throw, you can choose to make a Strength saving throw instead. |
^modifier-benefit
