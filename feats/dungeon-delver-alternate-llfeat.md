# Dungeon Delver (Alternate)
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, Player's Handbook p. 166, Alternate Rogue Expanded p. 5*  

You are as skilled as one can be at exploring dungeons and disarming deadly traps. You gain the benefits below:

**Dungeoneer Adept.** You gain proficiency in [Investigation](/rules/skills.md#Investigation), [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception), or [Thieves' Tools](/items/thieves-tools-xphb.md), and whenever you you make an ability check with any of the skills listed above, you can treat a roll of 7 or lower on the d20 as an 8.

**Thoughtful Steps.** Traveling at a fast past pace while in a dungeon does not impose a penalty on passive Perception or passive Investigation scores for you.

**Trap Sense.** You have advantage on all saving throws to resist traps and Resistance to all damage dealt by traps.
