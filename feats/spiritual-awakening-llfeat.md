# Spiritual Awakening
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded*  

You have spent many quiet hours learning to commune with the invisible world of Spirits. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Wisdom or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
- You learn the [mage hand](/spells/mage-hand-xphb.md) and [seance](/spells/seance-llspell.md) Cantrips, but they do not count against your number of Cantrips Known.  
- You learn the [unseen servant](/spells/unseen-servant-xphb.md) spell, and you can cast it at-will without expending a spell slot. When you do so, Wisdom is your spellcasting modifier, and the servant takes the shape of a local Spirit. you can only have one unseen servant conjured at a time. Should you conjure another, the first servant is instantly dispelled.
