# Metamagic Adept (Alternate)
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, Tasha's Cauldron of Everything p. 80, Alternate Sorcerer: Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: The ability to cast at least one spell

You have practiced and learned to manipulate your magic in strange and unique ways. You gain the benefits listed below:

- Increase one ability score that you use as a Spellcasting Ability by 1, up to a maximum of 20.  
- You learn a [Metamagic](/lists/list-optfeaturetype-as-mm.md) of your choice from those available to the Alternate Sorcerer ^[class/LaserLlama; Alternate Sorcerer.json]. You can use this Metamagic once (as if you had spent the minimum number of Sorcery Points) without spending Sorcery Points. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a short or long rest.
