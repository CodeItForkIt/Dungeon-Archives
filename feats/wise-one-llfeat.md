# Wise One
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded p. 5*  

You find ways to learn from every experience in life, always growing in wisdom. You gain the benefits on the table below that correspond to your current Wisdom modifier and lower.

Should your Wisdom modifier increase, the benefits that you gain from this Feat also increase with your modifier:

| Modifier | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| +1 | You gain proficiency with one set of [artisan's tools](/items/artisans-tools-xphb.md) of your choice. |
| +2 | You can make Wisdom ([Persuasion](/rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) checks in place of Charisma ([Persuasion](/rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) checks. |
| +3 | You gain proficiency in either [Insight](/rules/skills.md#Insight), [Medicine](/rules/skills.md#Medicine), or [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception). |
| +4 | When you make a Wisdom ([Insight](/rules/skills.md#Insight)) check, you treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8. |
| +5 | You have advantage on saving throws to resist enchantment and illusion spells. |
^modifier-benefit
