# Tactical Training
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, LaserLlama's Warlord Expanded p. 5*  

You have studied greater combat strategies that allow you to perform Tactical Exploits. You gain the following benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Wisdom, Charisma, or Intelligence by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Tactical Exploits Known.** You learn one of the following [Tactical Exploits](/lists/list-optfeaturetype-ll-tte.md) of your choice from the Warlord ^[class/LaserLlama; Warlord.json] Class:

|    |    |
|----|----|
| [attack order](/optional-features/attack-order-llexploits.md) | [steadfast order](/optional-features/steadfast-order-llexploits.md) |
| [defensive order](/optional-features/defensive-order-llexploits.md) | [support order](/optional-features/support-order-llexploits.md) |
| [maneuvering order](/optional-features/maneuvering-order-llexploits.md) | — |

**Issuing an Order.** Once per turn, when you take the [Attack](/rules/actions.md#Attack) action, you can use the Tactical Exploit in place of one attack. You can't use this Exploit more than once per turn unless you learn Tactical Exploits from a source other than this Feat.

**Repeatable.** You can choose this Feat multiple times. Each time you do, raise one of the ability scores above by one, and learn one new Tactical Exploit from the table above.
