# Scholar of Lore
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, LaserLlama's Savant Expanded p. 5*  

You have spent time learning everything there is to know about a specific area of study. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
- You master a [Scholarly Pursuit](/lists/list-optfeaturetype-ll-sp.md) of your choice from those available to the Savant ^[class/LaserLlama; Savant.json]. If the Pursuit requires an Intellect Die, you use a `d4` unless your Intellect Die is higher.
