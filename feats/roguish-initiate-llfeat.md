# Roguish Initiate
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, Alternate Rogue Expanded p. 5*  

You have spent time honing talents associated with the criminal underworld. You gain the following benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity or Intelligence by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Cunning Adept.** Choose one of the following actions: [Dash](/rules/actions.md#Dash), [Hide](/rules/actions.md#Hide), or [Use an Object](/rules/actions.md#Utilize). You can take that action as a bonus action on each of your turns.

**Thieves' Cant.** You learn to speak the jargon, write secret symbols of, and decode messages in Thieves' Cant.
