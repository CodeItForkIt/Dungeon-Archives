# Hardy Physique
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, Alternate Barbarian Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Constitution 13 or higher

You have hardened your physical body to absorb hits rather then dodge them. You can use your Constitution, in place of Dexterity, when calculating your Armor Class in armor.

You also have advantage on saving throws to avoid being moved against your will, [Grappled](/rules/conditions.md#Grappled), or knocked [Prone](/rules/conditions.md#Prone).
