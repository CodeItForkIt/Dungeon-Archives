# Mental Acuity
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Feats, LaserLlama's Savant Expanded p. 5*  

Your mind is a wonderful thing, capable of bursts of insight and mental fortitude. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
- You gain proficiency in two of the following skills: [Arcana](/rules/skills.md#Arcana), [History](/rules/skills.md#History), [Investigation](/rules/skills.md#Investigation), [Medicine](/rules/skills.md#Medicine), [Nature](/rules/skills.md#Nature), or [Religion](/rules/skills.md#Religion).  
- Choose any two skills from the list above. Whenever you make an ability check with either of the chosen skills you can treat a roll of 7 or lower on the d20 as an 8.
