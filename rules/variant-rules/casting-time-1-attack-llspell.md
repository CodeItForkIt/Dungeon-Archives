# Casting Time: 1 Attack
*Source: LaserLlama's Compendium of Spells* 

Spells with the casting time of "1 attack" can be cast in place of a single attack, whenever you could make a weapon attack during your turn. The spells cannot be cast in place of a spell attack, and to cast them as part of an opportunity attack, you need the [War Caster](/feats/war-caster-xphb.md) Feat or a similar feature.
