# Living Quill
*Wondrous item, common*  



This fanciful quill looks to be made from the feather of a bird of mysterious origin. As an action, you can speak the Quill's command word and touch the Quill to a piece of paper or parchment. As long as the Quill can hear you, it transcribes your words exactly. The Quill transcribes for 1 hour, but it stops sooner if you speak its command word again.

*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*
