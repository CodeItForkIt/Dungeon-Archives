# Rune of Evocation
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Item: a simple or martial melee weapon Feature

When you inscribe the Rune you imbue it with acid, cold, fire, poison, or lighting damage. Whenever the bearer hits with an attack with this Runic weapon, it deals additional damage of the imbued type equal to one roll of your Intellect Die.

## Invoking this Rune

When the bearer hits with an attack with this Runic weapon, it can Invoke the Rune to cause the attack to deal additional damage of the imbued damage type equal to three rolls of the Rune Scribe's Intellect Die.
