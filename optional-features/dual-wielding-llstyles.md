# Dual Wielding
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles)*  

**Prerequisite**: Strength or Dexterity 13 or higher

While two-weapon fighting, you make your off-hand attack as part of your [Attack](/rules/actions.md#Attack) action instead of your bonus action, and you add your ability modifier to the damage of this attack. You cannot also make an attack with your bonus action that turn.
