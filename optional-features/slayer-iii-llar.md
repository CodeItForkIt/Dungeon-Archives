# Slayer III
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 14th, Slayer I, II

When you hit a [Quarry](alternate-ranger-llar.md#Ranger's%20Quarry%20(Level%202)) with a weapon attack, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell save DC. On a failed save, it is [blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded), cannot speak, [deafened](/rules/conditions.md#Deafened), or [restrained](/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) (your choice) until the start of your next turn. You can only use this feature on your Quarry once per turn.
