# Spirit of the Forest
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Heart Sacred Focus Feature

You bind a minor Woodland Spirit that allows you to converse with wild creatures in your own tongue. You are always under the effect of [speak with animals](/spells/speak-with-animals-xphb.md) while you are conscious.

When you reach 7th level in this class, you are also always under the effect of [speak with plants](/spells/speak-with-plants-xphb.md) while you are conscious.
