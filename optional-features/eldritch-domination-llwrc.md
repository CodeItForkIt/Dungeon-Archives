# Eldritch Domination
*Source: Alternate Warlock p. 9*  

**Prerequisite**: 17th, Eldritch Familiar Feature

You can extend your will through the chains of your eldritch bond. Once in between each short or long rest, you can cast [dominate monster](/spells/dominate-monster-xphb.md) without expending a spell slot.

If you target a creature that is under the effect of your hold monster spell with dominate monster, it auto- -matically fails its saving throw against your dominate monster spell, but the effects of your hold monster spell instantly end.
