# Herbalist I
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: proficiency in [Medicine](/rules/skills.md#Medicine)

You have an extensive knowledge of plants and their healing properties. You gain proficiency with [herbalism kits](/items/herbalism-kit-xphb.md), and you automatically succeed on your Wisdom ([Medicine](/rules/skills.md#Medicine)) checks to stabilize creatures at 0 hit points if you use a herbalism kit.
