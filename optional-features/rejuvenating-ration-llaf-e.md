# Rejuvenating Ration
*Source: Alternate Fighter: Expanded p. 15*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Alternate Fighter

> [!note]
> Duration: instant

Upon consumption, the creature instantly gains the benefits of a short rest, including the ability to expend Hit Dice. At the end of its current turn, it gains 1 level of [Exhaustion](/rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) that can only be reduced by magic or completing a long rest.
