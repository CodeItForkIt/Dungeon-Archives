# Shield Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Shield Warrior](/optional-features/shield-warrior-llstyles.md) Feature

While wielding a [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md), you can use a bonus action on each of your turns to make a [Shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) attack, even if you do not take the [Attack](/rules/actions.md#Attack) action. You also add the Armor Class bonus from your shield to any Dexterity saving throws you make.
