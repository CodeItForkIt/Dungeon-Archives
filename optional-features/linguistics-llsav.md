# Linguistics
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You are a student of language and the art of the spoken word. You gain proficiency in [Persuasion](/rules/skills.md#Persuasion), and whenever you make a Charisma ([Persuasion](/rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) check, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to one roll of your Intellect Die.

You also know how to speak, read, and write a number of additional languages equal to your Intelligence modifier.
