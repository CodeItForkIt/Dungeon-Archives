# Physical Fitness
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You know that the key to a healthy mind is a healthy body. You gain proficiency in either [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics) or [Acrobatics](/rules/skills.md#Acrobatics). Whenever you make an ability check with that skill, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to one roll of your Intellect Die.

Also, you gain a climbing speed and a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and when you make a running jump, you add your Intelligence modifier to the distance.
