# Metamorphosis III
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 11 Psion, Metamorphosis Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ Feature

As a bonus action you can spend 3 Psi Points to transform as if you had cast [polymorph](/spells/polymorph-xphb.md) on yourself. While transformed in this way you retain your mental ability scores and personality.
