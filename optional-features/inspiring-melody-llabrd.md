# Inspiring Melody
*Source: Alternate Bard*  

As an action, you expend any number of Bardic Inspiration Dice (up to your Charisma modifier), and choose a creature who can hear you within 30 feet for each Bardic Inspiration Die spent. These creatures gain temporary hit points equal to the maximum roll of your Bardic Inspiration Die.
