# Spirit of the Vine
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Shaman

You bind a spirit of wild growth which lends you the power to restrain your foes. You can cast [ensnaring strike (alternate)](/spells/ensnaring-strike-alternate-llspell.md) at 1st-level, at will, without expending a spell slot.
