# Armor of the Ascetic
*Source: Alternate Monk p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 13

You exude an aura of peace. When you finish a short or long rest, you gain the benefits of the [sanctuary](/spells/sanctuary-xphb.md) spell, which last until the end of your next rest, or until you break the spell.
