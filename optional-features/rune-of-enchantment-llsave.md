# Rune of Enchantment
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Item: a bracelet, diadem, ring, or necklace Feature

Creatures will treat the bearer of this Runic object one stage friendlier than normal. For example, an indifferent creature might treat the bearer as a friend, or a hostile creature might treat the bearer indifferently. This special treatment instantly ends if the bearer attacks the creature.

## Invoking this Rune

As an action, the bearer can Invoke the Rune to cast either the [calm emotions](/spells/calm-emotions-xphb.md), [charm person](/spells/charm-person-xphb.md) or [command](/spells/command-xphb.md) spell, targeting a number of creatures equal to the Rune Scribe's Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1 creature).
