# Storm Rune
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 21*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 7

This Rune imbues the prophetic magic and might of Storm Giants. The Rune's wielder knows the [mighty leap](/optional-features/mighty-leap-llexploits.md) Exploit.

## Invoke

As a bonus action, you Invoke this Rune to enter a prophetic state that lasts for 1 minute. For its duration, when a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you can use your reaction to add or subtract your Exploit Die from the creature's roll.

Maintaining the prophetic state of this Rune requires your [concentration](/rules/conditions.md#Concentration) as if you were concentrating on a spell.
