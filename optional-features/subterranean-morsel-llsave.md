# Subterranean Morsel
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Sample: any Beast with a burrowing speed Feature

For 1 hour, the creature gains [darkvision](/rules/senses.md#Darkvision) out to 60 feet. If a creature already has darkvision, its radius grows by 30 feet.
