# Spirit of Draining
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 7*  

This Spirit siphons life from others to restore you. Once per turn when you hit a creature with this weapon, you can deal necrotic damage, and you gain temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier (min. of 1).
