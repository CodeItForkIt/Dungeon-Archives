# Brawling Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Brawling](/optional-features/brawling-llstyles.md) Feature

You gain a +1 bonus to the damage rolls of unarmed strikes. You also gain the benefits below while grappling creatures:

- You can drag [grappled](/rules/conditions.md#Grappled) creatures up to your full speed.  
- In place of an attack, you can make another [grapple](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) check against a target you are grappling. On a success, the target is [Restrained](/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) for the duration of that grapple.
