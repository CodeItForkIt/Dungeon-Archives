# Berserker Ration
*Source: Alternate Fighter: Expanded p. 15*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Alternate Fighter

> [!note]
> Duration: 1 minute

Upon consumption, the creature does not fall [unconscious](/rules/conditions.md#Unconscious) when reduced to 0 hit points. However, it still makes death saving throws as normal, and dies instantly if it fails three.
