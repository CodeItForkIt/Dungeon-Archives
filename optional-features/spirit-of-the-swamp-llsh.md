# Spirit of the Swamp
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman

You bind a Spirit of Disease to poison foes. When a creature fails its saving throw against Totemic Assault, you can reduce the damage by 1d8 and cause the target to be Poisoned until the beginning of your next turn.
