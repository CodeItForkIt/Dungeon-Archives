# Mystic Shot
*Source: Alternate Artificer Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 17 Alternate Artificer, a ranged weapon (requires attunement)

Item: ranged weapon (requires attunement) The wielder of this magic weapon can choose for any damage dealt by the weapon to be force damage, and it can be used as a spellcasting focus. They also know the [swift quiver (alternate)](/spells/swift-quiver-alternate-llspell.md) spell, and can cast it once without expending a spell slot.

The wielder of this magic weapon regains the ability to cast the spell without expending a spell slot at the following dawn.
