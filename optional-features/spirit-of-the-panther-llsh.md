# Spirit of the Panther
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 10*  

You bind a minor bestial spirit that grants you the steps of a panther. You gain proficiency in [Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth), and have advantage on Dexterity ([Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks to hide in natural environments.
