# Restoration III
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 11 Psion, Restoration Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ Feature

As an action, you can touch a willing creature and expend 2 Psi Points to end one of the following conditions affecting it: [blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded), [charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed), [deafened](/rules/conditions.md#Deafened), [frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened), [poisoned](/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned), or [stunned](/rules/conditions.md#Stunned).
