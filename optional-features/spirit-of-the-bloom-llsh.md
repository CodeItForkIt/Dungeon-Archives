# Spirit of the Bloom
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman, Soul Sacred Focus Feature

You bind a Spirit of Verdant Growth that enriches the lands around you. Once between each short or long rest, you can cast the [plant growth](/spells/plant-growth-xphb.md) spell without expending a spell slot.

Starting at 9th level, the 8-hour version of the [plant growth](/spells/plant-growth-xphb.md) spell takes effect on the land surrounding you each time you take a long rest, unless you wish to withhold this effect.
