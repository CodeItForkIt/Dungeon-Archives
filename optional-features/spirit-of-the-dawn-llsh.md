# Spirit of the Dawn
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Shaman

You bind a spirit of radiance which you can shield creatures from death. At the end of a long rest, you can cast [death ward](/spells/death-ward-xphb.md) on a creature of your choice, without expending a spell slot.
