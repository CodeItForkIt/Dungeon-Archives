# Spirit of Secrets
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman, Mind Sacred Focus Feature

You bind a Spirit of Knowledge that lets peer into the minds of others. Once between each short or long rest, you can cast the [detect thoughts](/spells/detect-thoughts-xphb.md) spell without expending a spell slot, or the normal verbal or somatic components of the spell.
