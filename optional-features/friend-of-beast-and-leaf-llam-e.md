# Friend of Beast and Leaf
*Source: Alternate Monk Expanded p. 2*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9

You can enter a meditative state to commune your Ki with that of the natural world around you. As an action, you can spend 5 Ki to enter this meditative state, which lasts for 10 minutes. At the end of your meditation, you gain the same information as if you had cast [commune with nature](/spells/commune-with-nature-xphb.md).
