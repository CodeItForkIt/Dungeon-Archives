# Spirit of the Rains
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 11*  

You bind a minor spirit of water that washes poison away like rain. You gain resistance to acid and poison damage, and you have advantage on saving throws to resist being [poisoned](/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned).
