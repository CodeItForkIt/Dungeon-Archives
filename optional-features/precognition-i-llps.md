# Precognition I
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

Psions who master Precognition use the mystical power of their minds to gain flashes of insight into the near future, and use these insights to better defend themselves from attacks.

You cannot be surprised unless you are [unconscious](/rules/conditions.md#Unconscious), asleep, or [incapacitated](/rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated), and you gain a bonus to your initiative rolls equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1).
