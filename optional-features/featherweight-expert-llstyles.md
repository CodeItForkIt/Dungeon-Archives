# Featherweight Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Featherweight Fighting](/optional-features/featherweight-fighting-llstyles.md) Feature

When [Featherweight Fighting](/optional-features/featherweight-fighting-llstyles.md), creatures have disadvantage on [opportunity attacks](/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack) against you, and you can take the [Disengage](/rules/actions.md#Disengage) action as a bonus action on each of your turns.
