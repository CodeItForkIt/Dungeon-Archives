# Slayer II
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, Slayer I Feature

Your tracking abilities have become supernaturally accurate. You can mark a creature as your [Quarry](/classes/alternate-ranger-llar.md#Ranger's%20Quarry%20(Level%202)) by studying signs of its passing, such as tracks, that were left within 24 hours.

Moreover, whenever you make a Wisdom ([Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception)) or a Wisdom ([Survival](/rules/skills.md#Survival)) check to locate or track your Quarry, you can treat a roll of 7 or lower on the `d20` as an 8.
