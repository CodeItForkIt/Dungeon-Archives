# Revitalizing Ration
*Source: Alternate Fighter: Expanded p. 15*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 3 Alternate Fighter

> [!note]
> Duration: instantaneous

Upon consumption, the creature ends one of the following conditions afflicting it: [Blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded), [Deafened](/rules/conditions.md#Deafened), or [Poisoned](/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned), or it can reduce its current level of [Exhaustion](/rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) by 1.

At 10th level, this Ration can also remove the [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed), [Frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened), [Paralyzed](/rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed), and [Stunned](/rules/conditions.md#Stunned) conditions.
