# Spirit of Midnight
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Shaman

You bind a sinister spirit which you can call to you. Once per long rest, you can expend a Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the [summon shadowspawn](/spells/summon-shadowspawn-tce.md) spell. Its Totem replaces the normal material components of this spell.

At 15th level, you use this between each short or long rest.
