# Ignan Morsel
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 10th; Sample: any Fire Elemental of CR 1 or higher Feature

For 1 hour, the creature gains resistance to fire damage, and it gains immunity to the [charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed) and [frightened](/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) conditions.
