# Naturalist II
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 9th, Naturalist I Feature

Your understanding grants you a mystical connection to the natural world. At the end of each short or long rest, you gain benefits as if you had cast the [commune with nature](/spells/commune-with-nature-xphb.md) spell.
