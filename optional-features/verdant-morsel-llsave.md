# Verdant Morsel
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Sample: any Plant of CR 1 or higher Feature

The creature is instantly cured of the following conditions: [blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded), [deafened](/rules/conditions.md#Deafened), [paralyzed](/rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed), [petrified](/rules/conditions.md#Petrified), [poisoned](/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned), a reduction to an ability score, or a reduction to its hit point maximum.
