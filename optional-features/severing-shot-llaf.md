# Severing Shot
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 24*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 10

The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it takes force damage equal to two Exploit Dice, and it cannot cast spells for 1 minute.

The creature can repeat this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
