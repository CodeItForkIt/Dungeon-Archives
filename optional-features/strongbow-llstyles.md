# Strongbow
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 4*  

**Prerequisite**: Strength 13 or higher

You can use your Strength modifier, in place of Dexterity, for attack and damage rolls with [longbows](/items/longbow-xphb.md) and [shortbows](/items/shortbow-xphb.md). When you do so, you gain a +1 to damage rolls with those weapons.
