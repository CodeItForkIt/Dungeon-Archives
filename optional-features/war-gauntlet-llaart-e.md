# War Gauntlet
*Source: Alternate Artificer Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Alternate Artificer; armored gauntlet and a martial weapon or shield

You attach a shield or martial weapon that can be wielded one-handed, or a shield, to this magical gauntlet. The wearer is proficient with the attached item, and the wearer cannot be disarmed of this item.

At 11th level this magic gauntlet grants a +1 bonus to Armor Class. This becomes a +2 bonus at 17th level.
