# Acidic Bile
*Source: Alternate Blood Hunter p. 6*  

**Prerequisite**: 7th

You can spew a corrosive acid forth from your mouth. You learn the [acid splash](/spells/acid-splash-xphb.md) cantrip, using Constitution as your spellcasting modifier. When you cast this cantrip you gain a bonus to the damage roll equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of +1).
