# Traditions
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You are a student of local cultures, politics, and traditions. You gain proficiency in [History](/rules/skills.md#History), and whenever you make an Intelligence ([History](/rules/skills.md#History)) check, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to one roll of your Intellect Die.

Whenever you make a Charisma check to interact with a local ruler or otherwise important figure and you incorporate a local custom or tradition, you have advantage on your roll.
