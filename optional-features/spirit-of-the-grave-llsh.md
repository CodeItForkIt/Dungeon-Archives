# Spirit of the Grave
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman

You bind a Spirit of Death which you can summon to your service. Once per long rest, you can expend a Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the [summon undead](/spells/summon-undead-xphb.md)  spell. This Totem replaces the normal material components of the spell.

At 15th level, you use this between each short or long rest.
