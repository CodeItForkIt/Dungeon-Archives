# First Aid
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You have learned basic medicinal techniques to aid your allies. You gain proficiency in [Medicine](/rules/skills.md#Medicine), and whenever you make a Wisdom ([Medicine](/rules/skills.md#Medicine)) check, you gain a bonus to your roll equal to one roll of your Intellect Die.

During a long rest, you can spend 1-hour using a [Healer's Kit](/items/healers-kit-xphb.md) to produce a number of [potions of healing](/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg.md) equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1). Any unused potions of healing expire 24 hours after you created them.
