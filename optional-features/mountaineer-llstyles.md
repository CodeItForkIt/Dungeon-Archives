# Mountaineer
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 4*  

**Prerequisite**: proficiency in [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics)

So long as you are not wearing heavy armor or a [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md), you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed, and you gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Class.

Also, whenever you take falling damage, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by twice your level.
