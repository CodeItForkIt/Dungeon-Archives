# Spirit of the Mystic
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman, Mind Sacred Focus Feature

You bind a spirit of secret knowledge that grants you the ability to peer into the minds of others. As an action, you can cast [detect thoughts](/spells/detect-thoughts-xphb.md) without expending a spell slot, or requiring the normal verbal or somatic components.

Once you cast detect thoughts in this way, you must finish a short or long rest before you can cast it in this way again.
