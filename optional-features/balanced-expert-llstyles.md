# Balanced Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Balanced Fighting](/optional-features/balanced-fighting-llstyles.md) Feature

When are wielding a versatile melee weapon and a [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md), you use the versatile weapon's larger damage die, and you can use your shield to do a [Shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) attack as a bonus action.
