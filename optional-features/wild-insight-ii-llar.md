# Wild Insight II
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 3rd, Wild Insight I Feature

Your bond with the wild allows you to conjure bestial spirits. You always have the [conjure familiar](/spells/conjure-familiar-llspell.md) spell prepared, but it doesn't count against the spells you prepare each day. When you cast conjure familiar, its creature type is always Fey.
