# Protection Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Protection](/optional-features/protection-llstyles.md) Feature

When a creature within 5 feet is forced to make a Dexterity saving throw, you can use your reaction to interpose a [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md), adding your Proficiency Bonus to its saving throw roll.

You can use this reaction after the creature rolls the d20, but before you know the outcome of its saving throw.
