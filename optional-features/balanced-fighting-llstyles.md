# Balanced Fighting
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 3*  

When wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to weapon damage rolls. You can use a [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md) and still gain this benefit.
