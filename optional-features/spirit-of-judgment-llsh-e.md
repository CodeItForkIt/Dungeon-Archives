# Spirit of Judgment
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Shaman

You bind a heavenly spirit which comes to your aid. Once per short or long rest, you can expend a Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the summon celestial spell. Its Totem replaces the normal material components of this spell.
