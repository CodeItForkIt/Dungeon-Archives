# Metamorphosis I
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

Psions who master Metamorphosis use the mystical power of their mind to manipulate the size and shape of their bodies.

Transformations from this Talent last for 10 minutes, and you concentrate on them as if concentrating on a spell.

You learn the [psionic strike](/spells/psionic-strike-llspell.md) cantrip, and when you manifest [psionic strike](/spells/psionic-strike-llspell.md), you can use your Intelligence modifier, in place of Strength or Dexterity, for the attack and damage rolls.
