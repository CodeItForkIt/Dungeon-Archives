# Classical Swordplay Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Classical Swordplay](/optional-features/classical-swordplay-llstyles.md) Feature

When are hit by an attack while using [Classical Swordplay](/optional-features/classical-swordplay-llstyles.md), you can use your reaction to add your Dexterity modifier to your Armor Class against that attack.
