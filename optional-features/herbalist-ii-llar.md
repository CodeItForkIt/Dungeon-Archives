# Herbalist II
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 3rd, Herbalist I Feature

You have learned to use natural plants and herbs to create healing salves. Over the course of a long rest, you can spend 1 hour using an [herbalism kit](/items/herbalism-kit-xphb.md) to create one [potion of healing](/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg.md). These potions of healing retain their potency until the end of your next long rest, at which point they become inert.
