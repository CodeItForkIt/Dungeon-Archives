# Voyager Boots
*Source: Alternate Artificer*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 11 Alternate Artificer, A pair of boots (requires attunement)

The wearer of these magical boots can use a bonus action to teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

At 17th level, the wearer can use the boots to teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
