# Melee Marksman
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 4*  

**Prerequisite**: Dexterity 13 or higher

Having a hostile creature within 5 feet of you does not impose disadvantage on your ranged weapon attacks, so long as you are attacking a target within 5 feet.

When you make a ranged weapon attack against a creature within 5 feet, it cannot target you with [opportunity attacks](/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack) for the rest of the turn regardless if you hit or missed your attack.
