# Boots of Destruction
*Source: Alternate Artificer Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 17 Alternate Artificer, pair of boots (requires attunement)

The wearer of these magical boots cannot be knocked prone or moved against its will. It also knows [destructive wave](/spells/destructive-wave-xphb.md), and can cast destructive wave once without expending a spell slot.

The wearer of these magic boots regains the ability to cast the spell without expending a spell slot at the following dawn.
