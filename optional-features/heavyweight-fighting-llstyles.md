# Heavyweight Fighting
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 3*  

**Prerequisite**: Strength 13 or higher

You gain a +1 bonus to your damage rolls with heavy melee weapons. When you hit a target with a heavy melee weapon attack, you can use your bonus action on that turn to make a [Shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) attack against the same target with advantage.
