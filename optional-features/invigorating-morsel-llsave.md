# Invigorating Morsel
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Sample: any Beast without a flying or swim speed Feature

The creature that eats this Morsel regains hit points equal to one roll of your Intellect Die + your Intelligence modifier.
