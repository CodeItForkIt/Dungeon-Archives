# Rider II
*Source: Alternate Ranger Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 3rd, Rider I Feature

You gain the [Mounted Warrior](/optional-features/mounted-warrior-llstyles.md) Fighting Style, and you can ride trained mounts that are equal to you in size or larger.

If you already know the Mounted Warrior Fighting Style you learn another Ranger Fighting Style of your choice.
