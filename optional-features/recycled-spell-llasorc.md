# Recycled Spell
*Source: Alternate Sorcerer p. 11*  

When you cast a spell and it fails, you can use your action on your next turn to regain Sorcery Points equal to half the Sorcery Points spent to to cast it (rounded down), including Metamagic.

A spell is considered to have failed if it misses its target, deals no damage, inflicts no conditions, or if it otherwise fails to create its intended effect.
