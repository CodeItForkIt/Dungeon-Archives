# Telekinesis II
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Psion, Telekinesis Ⅰ Feature

You can manifest two instances of [mage hand](/spells/mage-hand-xphb.md) at one time, so long as they remain within 30 feet of each other. Manifesting two [mage hands](/spells/mage-hand-xphb.md) at once requires your concentration.
