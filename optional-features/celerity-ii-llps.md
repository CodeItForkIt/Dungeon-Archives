# Celerity II
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Psion, Celerity Ⅰ Feature

Your walking speed increases by another 5 feet, and when you [Dash](/rules/actions.md#Dash), opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage.
