# Spirit of the Bear
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 10*  

You bind a minor Bestial Spirit granting you the ferocity of a bear. As a bonus action, you can sprout (or retract) ethereal claws from your fingers. When you make an unarmed strike with these claws, you use Wisdom, in place of Strength for the attack and damage rolls. On hit, they deal 1d6 damage.

Should you have two free hands, this d6 becomes a d8.
