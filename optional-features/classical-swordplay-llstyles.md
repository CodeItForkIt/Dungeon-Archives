# Classical Swordplay
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 3*  

**Prerequisite**: Dexterity 13 or higher

While wielding a single finesse weapon, no [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md), and not wearing heavy armor, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls with that weapon and a +1 bonus to your Armor Class.
