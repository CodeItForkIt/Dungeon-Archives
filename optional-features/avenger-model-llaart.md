# Avenger Model
*Source: Alternate Artificer*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 10 Alternate Artificer

When a creature within 25 feet of the Automaton attacks or forces another creature to make a saving throw, you can use a reaction to order this Automaton to move up to its walking speed and make a Slam attack against that creature. On hit, this attack deals a bonus 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
