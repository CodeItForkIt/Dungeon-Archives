# Rune of Divination
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 10th; Item: a wand, staff, robe, or spellcasting focus Feature

The bearer of this Runic object can use it as a spellcasting focus to cast the [comprehend languages](/spells/comprehend-languages-xphb.md), [detect magic](/spells/detect-magic-xphb.md) and [identify](/spells/identify-xphb.md) spells as rituals, without the material components.

## Invoking this Rune

As an action, the bearer can Invoke the Rune to empower their vision. For 1 hour, the bearer has [truesight](/rules/senses.md#Truesight), sees secret doors and traps hidden by magic, and can see into the Ethereal Plane, all out to a range of 120 feet.
