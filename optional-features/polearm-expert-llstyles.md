# Polearm Expert
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, [Polearm Fighting](/optional-features/polearm-fighting-llstyles.md) Feature

Whenever you take the [Attack](/rules/actions.md#Attack) action while using [Polearm Fighting](/optional-features/polearm-fighting-llstyles.md), you can use your bonus action on that turn to make a melee attack with the end of your weapon. On hit, it deals bludgeoning damage equal to `1d4`+ your Strength modifier.
