# Erupting Blast
*Source: Alternate Warlock p. 9*  

**Prerequisite**: 5th

When you damage a creature with Eldritch Blast you can use a bonus action to cause other targets of your choice within 5 feet of your target to take damage of the type of your Eldritch Blast equal to your Pact modifier (minimum of 1).
