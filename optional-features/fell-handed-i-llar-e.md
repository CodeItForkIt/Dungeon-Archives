# Fell Handed I
*Source: Alternate Ranger Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th

You pour the wrath of the wilds into each strike. Each time you hit your Quarry with an attack, your critical hit range is increased by 1. However, after you score a critical hit against it, your critical hit range against it returns to normal.

For example, after you hit your Quarry with one attack, your attacks score a critical hit against it on a roll of 19-20 on the d20. After two hits, a roll of 18-20 on the d20. But, if you score a critical hit on it, it returns to 20 on the d20.
