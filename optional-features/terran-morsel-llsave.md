# Terran Morsel
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 10th; Sample: any Earth Elemental of CR 1 or higher Feature

For 1 hour, the creature gains [tremorsense](/rules/senses.md#Tremorsense) out to a 15-foot radius, and it gains resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
