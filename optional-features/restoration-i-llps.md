# Restoration I
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 11*  

A rare Talent among Psions, those who master Restoration uses the mystic power of the mind to stimulate the healing process in themselves and others through force of will.

You learn the [spare the dying](/spells/spare-the-dying-xphb.md) cantrip. It doesn't count against your total number of Cantrips Known. When you manifest [spare the dying](/spells/spare-the-dying-xphb.md), it has a range of 30 feet.
