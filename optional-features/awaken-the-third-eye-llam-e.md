# Awaken the Third Eye
*Source: Alternate Monk Expanded p. 2*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 18

As an action, you can expend 8 Ki to enter a meditative state for 1 minute, granting you the benefits of the [foresight](/spells/foresight-xphb.md) spell.

While this effect is active, your Ki maximum is reduced by 8. You can end this effect early as an action on your turn.
