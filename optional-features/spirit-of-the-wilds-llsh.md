# Spirit of the Wilds
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman

You bind a Spirit of the Wilderness which you can call to your aid. Once per long rest, you can expend a Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the [summon beast](/spells/summon-beast-xphb.md) spell. Its Totem replaces the normal material components of this spell.

At 15th level, you use this between each short or long rest.
