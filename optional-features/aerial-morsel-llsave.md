# Aerial Morsel
*Source: LaserLlama's Savant Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: 10th; Sample: any Air Elemental of CR 1 or higher Feature

For 1 hour, the creature can take the [Dash](/rules/actions.md#Dash) action as a bonus action on each turn, and it can hold its breath indefinitely.
