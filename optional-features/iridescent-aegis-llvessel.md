# Iridescent Aegis
*Source: LaserLlama's Vessel*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 7 Vessel, Iridescent Shield Feature

When you use [Iridescent Shield](/optional-features/iridescent-shield-llvessel.md) on another creature and it still takes damage, you can choose to take half the remaining damage in its place as part of the same reaction.
