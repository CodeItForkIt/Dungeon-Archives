# Rite of Binding
*Source: Alternate Blood Hunter p. 12*  

As a bonus action, you invoke this Rite and force a Large or smaller creature that you can see within 30 feet to make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, its speed is reduced to 0 and it can't use reactions until the end of your next turn.

## Empowered Binding

You can target a creature of any size with this Rite, and it lasts for 1 minute. Creatures can repeat this saving throw at the end end each of their turns, ending the effects on a success.
