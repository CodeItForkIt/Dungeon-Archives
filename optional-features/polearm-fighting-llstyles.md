# Polearm Fighting
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 4*  

**Prerequisite**: Strength and Dexterity 11 or higher

You gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls with [glaives](/items/glaive-xphb.md), [halberds](/items/halberd-xphb.md), [pikes](/items/pike-xphb.md), [quarterstaffs](/items/quarterstaff-xphb.md), and [spears](/items/spear-xphb.md) so long as you are wielding only that weapon and no [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md). When doing so, creatures provoke an [opportunity attacks](/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack) from you when they enter or move within your reach with that weapon.
