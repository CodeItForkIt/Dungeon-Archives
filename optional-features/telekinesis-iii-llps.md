# Telekinesis III
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 11 Psion, Telekinesis Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ Feature

You learn [telekinesis](/spells/telekinesis-xphb.md), but it doesn't count against your total number of Spells Known. If you already know this spell you learn another Psion spell of your choice. When you manifest it you don't need to provide verbal or somatic components.
