# Striking Presence
*Source: LaserLlama's Vessel p. 12*  

The Spirit within empowers your force of personality. You gain proficiency in Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion.

While cloaked in your Spirit Mantle, you have advantage on ability checks you make with one skill from the list above, which you choose when you unlock this Unsealed Aspect.
