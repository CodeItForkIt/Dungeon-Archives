# Transposing Shot
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 24*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 10

The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, it takes force damage equal to two Exploit Dice, and you switch places with it without provoking [opportunity attacks](/rules/actions.md#Opportunity%20Attack).
