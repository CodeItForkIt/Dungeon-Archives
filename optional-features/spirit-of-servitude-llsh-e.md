# Spirit of Servitude
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 7 Shaman

You bind a minor Spirit of Loyalty to serve you in all things. The Totem you bind this Spirit to is always under the effects of the [tiny servant](/spells/tiny-servant-xge.md) spell. Should the Totem be reduced to zero hit points, it regenerates at the end of your next rest.
