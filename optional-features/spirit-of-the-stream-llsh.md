# Spirit of the Stream
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 11*  

You bind a minor Spirit of Water that grants you a protective aqueous coating. You have advantage on any ability check or saving throw you make to resist being grappled, restrained, or stunned, and you can hold your breath for up to 1 hour.
