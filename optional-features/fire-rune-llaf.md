# Fire Rune
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 21*  

This Rune imbues the fury of Fire Giants. This Rune's wielder knows the [imposing presence](/optional-features/imposing-presence-llexploits.md) Exploit.

## Invoke

When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can Invoke this Rune to conjure restraints of molten metal around it. It takes bonus fire damage equal to twice your Exploit Die and must succeed on a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it is [Restrained](/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) for 1 minute.

While Restrained it takes fire damage equal to twice your Exploit Die at the start of each of its turns. As an action, it can repeat the saving throw, ending this effect on a success.
