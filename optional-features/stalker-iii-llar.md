# Stalker III
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 9th, Stalker I, II

You cannot be tracked, even by magic. You are always under the effects of the [nondetection](/spells/nondetection-xphb.md) spell, and you can't be tracked by divination magic or magical means unless you wish to be.
