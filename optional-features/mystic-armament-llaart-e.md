# Mystic Armament
*Source: Alternate Artificer Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 17 Alternate Artificer, melee weapon (requires attunement)

The wielder's attacks with this magic weapon score a critical hit on a roll of 18-20 on the d20, and they can use the weapon as a spellcasting focus. It also knows the [steel wind strike (alternate)](/spells/steel-wind-strike-alternate-llspell.md) and can cast it once without expending a spell slot.

The wielder of this magic weapon regains the ability to cast the spell without expending a spell slot at the following dawn.
