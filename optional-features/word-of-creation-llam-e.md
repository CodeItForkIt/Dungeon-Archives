# Word of Creation
*Source: Alternate Monk Expanded p. 2*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 18

You can draw on your intimate knowledge of the Ki that flows through all to pluck the threads of creation. As an action, you can spend 7 Ki to produce the effects of the [divine word](/spells/divine-word-xphb.md) spell, using Wisdom as your Spellcasting modifier.

Once you use your Ki to cast [divine word](/spells/divine-word-xphb.md), you must finish a short or long rest before you can cast it in this way again.
