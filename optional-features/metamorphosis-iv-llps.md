# Metamorphosis IV
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 16 Psion, Metamorphosis Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ, ⅠⅠⅠ Feature

As a bonus action you can expend 5 Psi Points to transform, becoming Huge or Diminutive, gaining the following benefits:

## Huge

You gain temporary hit points equal to twice your Psion level + your Intelligence modifier, your reach increases by 10 feet, and your melee attacks deal `2d6` extra damage.

## Diminutive

You gain a +10 bonus to Dexterity ([Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks, attacks made against you have disadvantage, and you can fit through 1-inch gaps without squeezing. While you are Diminutive, you are too small to make weapon attacks.
