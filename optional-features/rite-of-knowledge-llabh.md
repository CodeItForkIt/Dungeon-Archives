# Rite of Knowledge
*Source: Alternate Blood Hunter p. 12*  

When you learn this Rite you gain knowledge of four Ritual spells. They can be from any spell list, but they must be of a level equal to your Proficiency Bonus or lower. However, to cast these Rituals, you must make a [Vital Sacrifice](alternate-blood-hunter-llabh.md#Vital%20Sacrifice%20(Level%202)).
