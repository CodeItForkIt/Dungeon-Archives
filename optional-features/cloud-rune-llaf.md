# Cloud Rune
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 21*  

This Rune imbues the cunning magic of Cloud Giants. This Rune's wielder knows the [mighty leap](/optional-features/mighty-leap-llexploits.md) Exploit.

## Invoke

When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet is targeted by an attack, you can use your reaction to Invoke this Rune to redirect the triggering attack at another target within its reach, other then itself.
