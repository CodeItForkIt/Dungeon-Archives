# Overcharged Spell
*Source: Alternate Sorcerer: Expanded p. 2*  

When you cast a spell with one target that requires you to make a spell attack roll, you can expend Sorcery Points to increase your critical hit range for this casting by 1 for each Sorcery Point.

For example, if you spent 3 Sorcery Points, the spell would score a critical hit on a roll of 17-20 on the d20.
