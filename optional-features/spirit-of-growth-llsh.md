# Spirit of Growth
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Shaman, Body Sacred Focus Feature

You bind a Spirit of Verdant Growth to temporarily increase your size. Once between each short or long rest, you can cast the enlarge version of the [enlarge/reduce](/spells/enlarge-reduce-xphb.md)on yourself wihtout expending a spell slot or requiring concentration.
