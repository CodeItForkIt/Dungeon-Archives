# Telekinesis I
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 11*  

Often considered the purest Talent a Psion can master, those who master Telekinesis use the mystical power of their mind to manipulate objects and creatures with only their thoughts.

You learn the [mage hand](/spells/mage-hand-xphb.md) cantrip, and when you manifest it you don't need to provide the verbal or somatic components. Your [mage hand](/spells/mage-hand-xphb.md) is invisible and can lift a number of pounds equal to 10 times your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 10).
