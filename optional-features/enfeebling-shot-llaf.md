# Enfeebling Shot
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 24*  

The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it takes necrotic damage equal to two Exploit Dice, and for 1 minute its weapon attacks deal half damage.

The creature can repeat this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
