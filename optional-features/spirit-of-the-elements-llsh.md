# Spirit of the Elements
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Shaman

You bind an elemental spirit which you can call to your aid. You can cast [summon elemental](/spells/summon-elemental-xphb.md) at the level of your Conduit Magic slots, without expending a spell slot or components.

Once you cast summon elemental in this way, you must finish a short or long rest before you can do so again.
