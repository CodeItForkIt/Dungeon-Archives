# Grasping Shot
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 24*  

The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it takes piercing damage equal to two Exploit Dice, and for 1 minute it is bound by thorns. For the duration, its speed is halved, and the first time it uses its speed to move on each turn it takes piercing damage equal to two Exploit Dice.

A creature can end this effect by using its action to make a successful Strength check against your Exploit save DC.
