# Versatile Fighting
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 4*  

**Prerequisite**: Strength or Dexterity 11 or higher

While wielding a single versatile weapon and no [shield](/items/shield-xphb.md), you gain a +1 bonus to your attack rolls with that weapon. While doing so, you can use your bonus action to make a [grapple](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) or [shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) attack, or to take the [Use an Object](/rules/actions.md#Utilize) action.
