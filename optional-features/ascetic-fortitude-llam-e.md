# Ascetic Fortitude
*Source: Alternate Monk Expanded p. 2*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9

As a reaction when you take damage, you can expend 2 Ki to reduce the incoming damage by an amount equal to two rolls of your Marital Arts die + your Wisdom modifier.
