# Spirit of the Cave
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Shaman

You bind a Spirit of Earth that lends you its connection to the ground. While you are touching the ground with a bare feet or hand, you gain tremorsense out to a 30-foot radius, allowing you to sense the presence of any creatures or objects that are touching the ground within the area.
