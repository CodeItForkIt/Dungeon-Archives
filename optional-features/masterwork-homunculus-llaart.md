# Masterwork Homunculus
*Source: Alternate Artificer*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 17 Alternate Artificer

You have discovered the secrets to the creation of life. You learn the [clone](/spells/clone-xphb.md) spell. It counts as an Artificer spell for you, but it doesn't count against your total number of Spells Known. You can cast clone without expending a spell slot as long as you have the material components available. Your clone only takes 12 days to reach maturity, but you can only have one.

If you replace this Infusion with another at any point before your clone reaches maturity, the spell immediately fails.
