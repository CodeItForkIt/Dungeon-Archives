# Brawling
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 3*  

**Prerequisite**: proficiency in [Athletics](/rules/skills.md#Athletics)

On hit, your unarmed strikes deal damage equal to `1d6` + your Strength modifier. When you have both hands free, you can make a single unarmed strike, [Shove](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md), or [Grapple](/rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md) as a bonus action on each of your turns.
