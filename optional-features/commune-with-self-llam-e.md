# Commune with Self
*Source: Alternate Monk Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9

You have gained the ability to contact the Ki of your previous lives to gain information. As an action, you can spend 5 Ki to enter a meditative state, which lasts for 10 minutes, and consult your previous lives. At the end of your meditation, you gain the same information as if you had cast [commune](/spells/commune-xphb.md).
