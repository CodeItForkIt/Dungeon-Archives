# Blind Warrior
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 3*  

**Prerequisite**: proficiency in [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception)

You have [Blindsight](/rules/senses.md#Blindsight) with a range in feet equal to 5 times your Proficiency Bonus. In that range, you can see [invisible](/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) targets and anything that isn't behind total cover or hidden from you.
