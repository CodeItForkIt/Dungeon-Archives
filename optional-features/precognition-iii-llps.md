# Precognition III
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 11 Psion, Precognition Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ Feature

As a bonus action on your turn, you can spend 3 Psi Points to enter a heightened precognitive state for 1 minute. While in this state, attacks targeting you cannot be made with advantage, and critical hits become normal hits. You must concentrate on this ability as if concentrating on a spell.
