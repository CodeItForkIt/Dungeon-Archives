# Slowing Strike
*Source: Alternate Monk p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5

Once per turn when you hit a creature with a melee Martial Arts attack, you can spend 1 Ki to assault its Ki flow, forcing it to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it suffers the effects of the [slow](/spells/slow-xphb.md) spell until the start of your next turn.

You do not need to concentrate on this effect.
