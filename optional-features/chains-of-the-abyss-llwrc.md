# Chains of the Abyss
*Source: Alternate Warlock p. 9*  

**Prerequisite**: 15th, Eldritch Familiar Feature

You can dominate other creatures with the eldritch chains of your Pact. You learn the [hold monster](/spells/hold-monster-xphb.md) spell. You can cast it without expending a spell slot or material components when you target an aberration, celestial, elemental, fey, or fiend.

Once a creature succeeds on its saving throw against this spell it is immune to the effects of hold monster until the next dawn, unless you expend a Pact Magic spell slot to cast it.
