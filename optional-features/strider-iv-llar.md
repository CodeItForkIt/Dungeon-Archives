# Strider IV
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 14th, Strider I, II, III

You move through the world unhindered by even the most powerful magic and restraints. You are always under the effects of the [freedom of movement](/spells/freedom-of-movement-xphb.md) spell while conscious.
