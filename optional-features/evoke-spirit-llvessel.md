# Evoke Spirit
*Source: LaserLlama's Vessel p. 13*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 7 Vessel

You can release a minor form of the Spirit sealed within you. You learn the [conjure familiar](/spells/conjure-familiar-llspell.md) spell. It is a Vessel spell for you, but it does not count against your total number of Spells Known. You also learn to cast this spell as a Ritual.

When you cast it, your Familiar resembles a Tiny version of your Sealed Spirit; it shares the creature type of your Archon, can attack on its turn, and uses the Spirit Familiar stat block
