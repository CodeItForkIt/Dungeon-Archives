# Spirit of Binding
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Shaman

You can quickly bind a spirit to regain some of your power. Once per long rest, you can use an action to regain one of your expended Conduit Magic spell slots.
