# Beguiling Shot
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 24*  

The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it takes psychic damage equal to two Dice and until the start of your next turn, it is [Charmed](/rules/conditions.md#Charmed) by a creature of your choice that it can see. This effect instantly ends if you or your allies do anything harmful or hostile toward it.
