# Umbral Shot
*Source: Alternate Fighter p. 24*  

The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, it takes psychic damage equal to two Exploit Dice, and for 1 minute it is [Blinded](/rules/conditions.md#Blinded) by visual hallucinations.

The creature can repeat this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
