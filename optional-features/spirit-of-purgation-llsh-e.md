# Spirit of Purgation
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Shaman

You bind an infernal spirit to unleash on your foes. Once per short or long rest, you can expend a Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the [summon greater demon](/spells/summon-greater-demon-xge.md) spell, using its Totem in place of the spell's normal material components.
