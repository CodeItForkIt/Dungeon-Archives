# Slayer I
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 3rd, Naturalist I Feature, proficiency in [Perception](/rules/skills.md#Perception)

When you hit a creature with an attack, you can mark it as your Quarry, expending a use of Ranger's Quarry as normal, and applying the bonus damage and other benefits on hit.

Finally, you can add double your Proficiency Bonus to any Perception checks that you make.
