# Spirit of Peace
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Shaman, Soul Sacred Focus Feature

You bind an ancient Spirit of Life which deters your enemies. At the end of each short or long rest, you gain the benefits of the [sanctuary](/spells/sanctuary-xphb.md) spell, which last until the end of your next short or long rest, or until you break the spell as normal.
