# Superior Technique
*Source: Alternate Martial Multiclassing (Fighting Styles) p. 5*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, at least one Exploit Known Feature

You learn one [1st-degree Martial Exploit](/lists/list-optfeaturetype-ll-1me.md) from those available to the Alternate Fighter, but it doesn't count against your total Exploits Known. You also gain one additional Exploit Die.
