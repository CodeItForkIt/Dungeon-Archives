# Wildfire Cannon
*Source: Alternate Artificer Expanded*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Alternate Artificer; gauntlet, arcane focus, or metal rod

As an action, the wielder of this magical object can expend Charges and use your Artificer Spellcasting modifier to cast these spells: firebolt (0 Charges), [burning hands](/spells/burning-hands-xphb.md) (1 Charge), [scorching ray](/spells/scorching-ray-xphb.md) (2 Charges), or [fireball](/spells/fireball-xphb.md) (3 Charges).

This magical item has a number of Charges equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1 Charge), and it regains all of its expended Charges each day at dawn.
