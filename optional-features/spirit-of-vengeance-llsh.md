# Spirit of Vengeance
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9 Shaman

You bind a vengeful Fey Spirit which you can call to your aid. Once per long rest, you can expend one Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the [summon fey](/spells/summon-fey-xphb.md) spell. This Totem replaces the normal material components of this spell.

At 15th level, you use this between each short or long rest.
