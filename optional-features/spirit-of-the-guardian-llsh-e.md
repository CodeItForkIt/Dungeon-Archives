# Spirit of the Guardian
*Source: LaserLlama's Shaman Expanded p. 12*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 15 Shaman

You bind a powerful Guardian Spirit to come to your defense. Once per short or long rest, you can expend a Conduit Magic spell slot to cast the [summon construct](/spells/summon-construct-xphb.md) spell. This Totem replaces the normal material components of this spell.

When you cast [summon construct](/spells/summon-construct-xphb.md) in this way, you can only summon the Clay or Stone Construct Spirit.
