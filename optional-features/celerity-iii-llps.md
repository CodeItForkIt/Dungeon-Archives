# Celerity III
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 11 Psion, Celerity Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ Feature

Your walking speed increases by an additional 5 feet. Also, when you [Dash](/rules/actions.md#Dash), you ignore the effects of all difficult terrain, and you can move along vertical surfaces and across liquids without falling during your movement on that turn.
