# Metamorphosis II
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5 Psion, Metamorphosis Ⅰ Feature

As a bonus action you can expend 1 Psi Point to transform, becoming Large or Tiny, gaining the respective benefits:

## Large

You gain temporary hit points equal to your Psion level + your Intelligence modifier, your reach increases by 5 feet, and your melee attacks deal `1d4` extra damage on hit.

## Tiny

You have advantage on Dexterity ([Stealth](/rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks and you can fit through 6-inch gaps without having to squeeze.
