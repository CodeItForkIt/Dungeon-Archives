# Celerity IV
*Source: LaserLlama's Psion p. 10*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 16 Psion, Celerity Ⅰ, ⅠⅠ, ⅠⅠⅠ Feature

When you make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage from a spell or effect, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the save, and only half damage if you fail.

Also, when you make a Dexterity check or saving throw, you can use your Intelligence score, in place of Dexterity.
