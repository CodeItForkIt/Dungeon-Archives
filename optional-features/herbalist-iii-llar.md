# Herbalist III
*Source: Alternate Ranger*  

**Prerequisite**: 6th, Herbalist I, II

When you use your [herbalism kit](/items/herbalism-kit-xphb.md) to craft [potions of healing](/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg.md) during a long rest, you craft a number of potions of healing equal to 1 + your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 2).
